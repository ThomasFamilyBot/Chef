**Dry the wieners**

- Line a plate with paper towels, spread the cocktail wieners on it and pat dry. Batter won't cling to wet wieners.

**Batter**

- In one bowl, whisk the cornmeal, flour, baking powder and salt.
- In another, whisk the milk, egg and honey. Stir the wet into the dry until combined.

**Fry**

- In a heavy pot or Dutch oven, heat the oil to 350°F. Keep it from climbing much past 350°F.
- Working in small batches, dip each wiener in the batter with a fork (or on a toothpick, and fry it on the toothpick), tap off the excess, and lower into the oil.
- Fry until golden brown all over, 2-3 minutes. Lift out with a slotted spoon onto paper towels. Repeat with the rest.
- Serve warm.

**Notes**

- If the batter slides off, dust the dried wieners lightly with flour first.
- Leftovers keep 3 days in the fridge; reheat at 375°F for about 5 minutes, or in the air fryer.
