**Toast the bread**

- Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a large baking dish.
- Spread the bread from `1 -> Toast the bread` on a baking sheet and toast, stirring, until lightly browned and crisp, about 25 minutes.

**Vegetables**

- Meanwhile, melt the stick of butter in a large, deep skillet. Pour half into a small bowl and reserve for the top.
- Add the celery, carrots and onion and cook over medium-high heat (pan surface 375-425°F), stirring now and then, until softened and just starting to brown, about 8 minutes. Scrape into a large bowl.

**Sausage**

- Add the sausage to the skillet in lumps and cook over medium-high heat, breaking it up, until lightly browned and cooked through, about 6 minutes.

**Herbs & deglaze**

- Return the vegetables to the skillet, add the sage and thyme and cook 1 minute.
- Add 1 cup of the stock and cook, scraping up the browned bits, until nearly evaporated, about 5 minutes.

**Assemble & bake**

- Scrape the sausage mixture into the large bowl, add the toasted bread and the remaining 2 cups stock, and stir until evenly moistened. Season with salt and pepper.
- Spread in the buttered dish and brush with the reserved melted butter.
- Bake in the center of the oven until heated through and the top is browned and crisp, about 1 hour. Let stand 10 minutes before serving.

**Notes**

- Make ahead: assemble through the brushing step and refrigerate overnight. Bring to room temperature before baking.
- There is no egg, so it bakes up loose rather than sliceable (a reviewer's complaint). Add 2 beaten eggs with the stock if you want it to hold together.
