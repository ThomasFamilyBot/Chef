**Soffritto**

- Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat (pan surface about 300-350°F).
- Add the onion, carrots, celery, salt and several grinds of pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, 8 minutes, until the vegetables begin to soften.

**Simmer**

- Add the garlic, tomatoes, beans, green beans, broth, bay leaves, oregano and thyme.
- Cover and simmer (185-205°F) for 20 minutes.

**Pasta**

- Stir in the pasta and cook, uncovered, 10 more minutes, until the pasta is cooked through. Fish out the bay leaves.

**Finish**

- Season to taste. Serve with the parsley, red pepper flakes and Parmesan, if using.

**Notes**

- Swaps: fennel for celery, zucchini or yellow squash for green beans, farro or barley for pasta. Add spinach or kale near the end.
- Keeps up to 4 days. If making ahead, cook the pasta separately so it doesn't swell in the soup.
- Nutrition counts 1/4 cup Parmesan for serving.
