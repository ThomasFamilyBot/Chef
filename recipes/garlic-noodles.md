**Make the garlic sauce**

- Melt the butter in a wok or saucepan over medium heat (pan surface about 300-350°F).
- Add the garlic and cook, stirring, until fragrant but not browned, about 2 minutes.
- Add the oyster sauce, soy sauce and fish sauce from `1 -> Garlic sauce` and stir to combine. Remove from the heat.

**Cook the pasta**

- Meanwhile, bring 1 1/2 inches of water to a boil (212°F) in a 12-inch skillet or sauté pan over high heat. (Or heat just enough water to cover the spaghetti in a large Dutch oven or saucepan.)
- Add the pasta, stir a few times so it doesn't clump, and cook, stirring occasionally, until just shy of al dente, about 2 minutes short of the package time.

**Finish**

- Using tongs, move the pasta into the garlic sauce with whatever water clings to it. Keep the pasta water in the skillet.
- Turn the heat to high, add the cheese, and toss vigorously with a wooden spatula or spoon until the sauce is creamy and emulsified, about 30 seconds.
- Too watery: let it keep reducing. Greasy: splash in more pasta water and let it re-emulsify.
- Stir in the scallions, if using, and serve immediately.

**Notes**

- Adapted from Kenji's *The Wok*, after the garlic noodles Helene An created at Thanh Long in San Francisco.
- Good with seafood: stir-fry raw, shell-on shrimp with the garlic from the start, or finish with crab, lobster, mentaiko, tobiko or ikura.
