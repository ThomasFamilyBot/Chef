**Day 1 (or the morning of)**

**Braise the lamb**

- Sprinkle the shanks with the 1 tsp salt and chill, up to overnight.
- In a Dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat (pan surface 375-425°F) and brown the shanks on all sides; this can take up to 30 minutes. Cover with the lid to limit spattering, and sprinkle with more salt and pepper as they go.
- Lower the heat and add the water or stock, the quartered lemon, the oregano sprigs and the garlic. Cover and simmer over low heat (liquid 180-200°F, a bare simmer) for 1 1/2-2 hours, turning the shanks and adding water 1/4 cup at a time as needed.
- Done when the meat is falling off the bones (about 200°F internal). Pull the meat off, shred it, and moisten it with some of the braising liquid. ([Braised Lamb Shanks with Lemon & Oregano](https://www.teaandfog.com/home/2018/1/braised-lamb-shanks-with-lemon-and-oregano))

**Day 2**

**Vinaigrette**

- In a large bowl, whisk the lemon juice with 1 tsp oregano leaves and enough olive oil to form a vinaigrette. Season with salt and pepper.

**Harissa yogurt**

- Stir the harissa into the yogurt in a small bowl.

**Assemble**

- For one giant salad, pile the greens into the bowl on top of the dressing; otherwise divide the dressing among smaller bowls and add greens to each.
- Dollop the harissa yogurt over the greens. Top with the shredded lamb, cucumber, pickled onion and radishes, and shower with as much feta as you dare.
- Toss to coat everything in the dressing. Serve immediately.

**Notes**

- Souvla style: the original is built from leftover braised lamb, so braise a day ahead or use any leftover lamb.
- The pickled red onion is store-bought or your own; the source gives no recipe.
