**Season the lamb**

- Pat the lamb dry and season generously with salt and pepper. Let stand at room temperature 1 hour.
- Heat the oven to 400°F.

**Herb crumbs**

- In a bowl, stir together the bread crumbs, garlic, parsley, rosemary, thyme, olive oil and a little salt and pepper.

**Coat & roast**

- Coat the lamb on all sides with the mustard. Pack some of the crumb mixture onto the underside, set the lamb fat side up on a rack in a large roasting pan, and pack the rest onto the top and sides.
- Roast 30 minutes, then lower the oven to 350°F and roast until a probe in the thickest part, away from the bone, reads 130°F for medium-rare, 1 1/4 to 1 1/2 hours more.

**Rest & carve**

- Move to a carving board, tent loosely with foil and rest 20 minutes.
- Carve into thin slices onto a warmed platter and serve right away.

**Notes**

- A reviewer used crumbs from Asiago bread and spread the leftover crumbs under the roast, then served them as a topping.
