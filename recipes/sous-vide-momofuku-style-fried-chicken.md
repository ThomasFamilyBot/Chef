**Brine and sous vide**

- Set the sous vide bath to 150°F.
- Dissolve 1/2 cup sugar and 1/2 cup salt in 4 cups warm water. Add the chicken and refrigerate 1 hour.
- Lift the chicken out of the brine into a zip-top or vacuum bag, seal, and cook in the bath 3 hours.

**Korean glaze**

- While the chicken cooks, combine the garlic, ginger, serrano, rice vinegar, soy sauce, canola oil, sesame oil, 1 1/2 TBLS sugar and black pepper. Shake or stir and set aside.

**Southern dredge (half the chicken)**

- Take the chicken out of the bath. Soak half the pieces in buttermilk 15 minutes; set the other half aside naked.
- Mix the flour, cornmeal, 1 TBLS salt and garlic powder. Dredge the buttermilk pieces until covered, then rest 15 minutes so the coating sets.

**Fry**

- Heat oil in a tall pot or fryer to 375°F.
- Fry the dredged pieces 6-8 minutes, until golden and crisp (the chicken is already cooked; you're after color). Drain on paper towels.
- Fry the naked pieces 6-8 minutes until golden and crisp, then toss them immediately in the glaze.
- Serve both styles hot, side by side.

**Notes**

- Chang's idea: one bird two ways, half Southern, half Korean with a sticky-sweet glaze. Sous vide means no fear of under- or overcooking in the fryer.
- Nutrition counts the brine, soak, dredge and frying oil at the house partial-use rates.
