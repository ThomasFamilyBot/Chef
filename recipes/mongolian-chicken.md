**Dredge the chicken**

- Mix the sliced chicken with 1 TBLS oil. Dredge in the 1/4 cup cornstarch, shaking off the excess so it's just lightly coated.

**Fry**

- Heat 1/3 cup oil in a wok over high heat (pan surface 450-500°F). If the chicken has gone moist again, dredge it once more.
- Just before the oil smokes, spread the chicken in the wok in one layer and fry 1 minute, until browned and crisp. Turn and fry 30 seconds more. Drain on paper towels; it should be crusty on all sides.

**Sauce**

- Pour off all but 1 TBLS of the oil and set the wok over medium-high heat (375-425°F). Fry the ginger 20 seconds, until fragrant.
- Add the dried chiles (if using), garlic and scallion whites. Stir-fry 15 seconds, then add the soy sauce, brown sugar and water. Bring to a simmer, stirring until the sugar dissolves, and simmer 2 minutes.

**Thicken**

- Stir 2 TBLS cornstarch into 2 TBLS water, then stir the slurry slowly into the sauce until it coats the back of a spoon.
- Add the chicken and scallion greens and toss about 10 seconds. There should be almost no loose liquid; if there is, add a little more slurry and turn up the heat. Serve.

**Notes**

- Thighs are harder to overcook than breast. No marinating needed.
- Gluten-free with gluten-free soy sauce. Leftovers keep 4 days.
