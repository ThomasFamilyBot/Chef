**Soak the ribs**

- Soak the short ribs in cold water for 1 hour or more. Discard the water and rinse the meat.

**Braise**

- Heat the oven to 350°F.
- Put the ribs from `1 -> Soak` in a large braiser or Dutch oven. Add everything from `2 -> Braise`, using enough of the water to mostly cover the ribs.
- Bring to a boil (212°F) on the stove, cover with a lid and braise in the oven for 3 hours, until the ribs are completely tender (a probe slides in with no resistance, around 200-205°F internal).
- Check the liquid as it cooks and add water as needed so the ribs stay mostly submerged.

**Add the vegetables**

- When the meat is tender, add everything from `3 -> Vegetables`. Cover again and cook in the oven 30 minutes more.

**Serve**

- Spoon the stew over the steamed rice and top with the scallions and sesame seeds from `4 -> Serve`.

**Notes**

- Momofuku says this is inspired by Sun Nong Dan, a galbi spot in Los Angeles, and suggests topping it with shredded mozzarella for the full Sun Nong Dan experience.
- The seasoned salt amount is as the original gives it. It is a blend, so don't swap in the same amount of plain salt.
