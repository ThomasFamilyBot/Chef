**Melt the butter**

- Melt the butter in a small saucepan over low heat and set aside to cool.
- Turn on the waffle cone maker to preheat.

**Batter**

- In a medium bowl, whisk the egg whites and cream until combined.
- Add the sugar, cinnamon, salt, vanilla bean paste and almond extract and whisk until well combined.
- Whisk in the melted butter.
- Add the flour and whisk only until just combined and smooth.

**Cook and shape the cones**

- Spray both grids of the waffle cone maker with nonstick spray.
- Scoop 1/4 cup of batter into the center and cook per the manufacturer's instructions. On a Chef's Choice Waffle Cone Express, color setting 3 for 2-3 minutes works.
- Lay a dish towel next to the machine. When the round is cooked to your liking, quickly move it to the towel.
- Set the point of the cone form near the edge of the round, lying across its diameter. Using the cloth to protect your fingers, wrap one edge onto the form and roll forward until the round is fully wrapped.
- Hold the cone seam-side down against the cloth, gently but firmly, about 15 seconds, and pinch the tip closed so it seals.
- Once it holds its shape, slide it off the form and cool upright (in a tall glass) or on its side on a wire rack.
- Fill with ice cream and serve.

**Notes**

- Best the same day, slightly warm. Keeps in an airtight container up to 5 days.
- Use 3 TBLS of batter per cone for 8-9 smaller cones.
- Waffle bowls: same batter; drape each hot round into a small bowl and cool it there. Double them up for a sturdier bowl.
- Source's own estimate: 244 calories per cone.
