**Season the chickpeas**

- Heat the oven to 500°F.
- In a broiler-safe 9x13-inch pan, toss the chickpeas with 1 TBLS of the oil, half the garlic, and the cumin and/or red pepper flakes from `1 -> Chickpeas`. Season with salt.

**Roast, then broil the sausages**

- Set the sausages from `2 -> Sausages` on top and drizzle with the remaining 1 TBLS oil.
- Roast until the sausages are firm but not yet golden, about 10 minutes. Cold sausages may need a minute or two longer.
- Turn on the broiler (or move the pan under it, 3-4 inches from the element) and broil until the sausages are browned in spots and cooked through (160°F internal for pork; 165°F for chicken or turkey sausage), about 5 minutes.

**Make the garlic vinegar**

- Meanwhile, mix the remaining garlic with the cider vinegar and a pinch of salt (`3 -> Garlic vinegar`).

**Finish**

- When the sausages are golden on top (the undersides won't color much), move them to a platter.
- Toss the spinach from `4 -> Finish` into the hot chickpeas until it wilts.
- Drizzle with the garlic vinegar and toss well. Taste; add more salt or vinegar if needed.
- Serve the chickpeas and spinach with the sausages.

**Notes**

- Any sausage works: pork, chicken or turkey, sweet or spicy.
