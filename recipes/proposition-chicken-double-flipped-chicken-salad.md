**Chicken**

- Pull or slice the cooked chicken breast into bite-size pieces. A store rotisserie bird or leftover chicken is the easy route; Proposition Chicken's "flipped" chicken is slow-cooked rotisserie.

**Salad**

- Pile the greens in a wide bowl. Add the tomatoes and cucumber.

**Finish**

- Top with the chicken, shower with the parmesan and finish with plenty of black pepper.

**Notes**

- This is a double portion of chicken (the app entry was "Flipped Chicken salad w extra Chx").
- The restaurant version also has torn mint and a black-pepper parmesan dressing. For the dressing, any creamy parmesan or Caesar-style dressing with lots of cracked pepper works; it isn't counted in the nutrition, so add about 70-80 calories per tablespoon.
- Nutrition counts the chicken at its cooked weight. The family's app total for the same ingredients was 225 calories using a fat-free deli-style breast; plain cooked breast is higher.
