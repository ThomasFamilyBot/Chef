**Za'atar breadcrumbs**

- Heat the oven to 350°F. Pulse the bread cubes in a food processor until finely chopped.
- Spread on a small baking sheet, drizzle with 1 TBLS oil, the za'atar and salt, toss, and bake 10-12 minutes, until golden and crisp.

**Quinoa**

- Bring the broth and quinoa to a boil in a small pot. Cover, reduce to low, and simmer about 15 minutes. Off the heat, leave covered 10 minutes more.

**Spicy broccoli**

- Tip the breadcrumbs off the sheet. Toss the broccoli on it with 1/2 TBLS oil, 1/2 tsp umami seasoning and the red pepper flakes, spread in one layer, and roast about 15 minutes.

**Chicken**

- Sprinkle 1 TBLS umami seasoning over both sides of the chicken. Heat a large pan (cast iron) over medium-high (pan surface 375-425°F), add 1 TBLS oil and the chicken, and cook 3 minutes per side (160°F). Rest 5 minutes, then cube.

**Pesto vinaigrette**

- While the chicken rests, blend the basil, 1/4 cup oil, vinegar, garlic and 1 tsp umami seasoning until smooth. Thin with 1-2 TBLS water if you want it to stretch.

**Bowl**

- Divide the spinach, quinoa, tomatoes, breadcrumbs, broccoli, chicken and Parmesan among four bowls and dress with the vinaigrette. Hot sauce if you like.

**Notes**

- Shortcuts: pre-cooked quinoa (about 1 1/2 cups), rotisserie chicken, or skip the breadcrumbs.
- Keeps about 4 days; store the vinaigrette separately.
