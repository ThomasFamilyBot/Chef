**Blend**

- Put everything from `1 -> Blend` in the blender, milk first so the blades can catch, then the honey, banana and fruit.
- Blend at full speed until completely smooth, 30-45 seconds. Scrape down the sides once if the fruit rides up.
- Too thick to pour? Add a splash more milk and blend again. Too thin? Add a few more frozen fruit pieces.
- Pour into an 8 oz glass and drink right away.

**Notes**

- Frozen strawberries and mango make it cold and thick without ice. With fresh fruit, add 2-3 ice cubes.
- The riper the banana, the sweeter the smoothie; taste before adding the honey.
- Makes about 8 oz. Double everything for two glasses.
