**Base**

- In a medium bowl, whisk the milk and sugar until the sugar dissolves.
- Add the yogurt and whisk until smooth and creamy. It's ready for the machine when the mixture clings to the spoon.

**Churn**

- With the ice cream maker running, pour in the base. It freezes fast; 10-15 minutes at most.

**Toppings**

- Scoop into bowls. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with flaky salt and pomegranate seeds.

**Notes**

- Souvla style: the olive oil and salt are the point; don't skip them.
- Source gives no yield; the base is about 3 1/4 cups, so figure four servings.
