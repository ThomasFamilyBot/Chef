**Crisp the bottoms**

- Burner on H2 (low). Add the oil and the dumplings, flat side down.
- Cook 5 minutes, until the bottoms are lightly toasted.

**Steam**

- Pour in 1/2 cup broth or water, cover, and cook 8 minutes.

**Notes**

- Nutrition counts the oil and broth only; add the dumplings from the package label.
