**Cheese**

- Heat a small nonstick skillet over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F). Spread half the cheese in an even layer over the bottom.
- Without disturbing it, cook until the cheese begins to bubble, about 1 minute.

**Egg**

- Crack 1 egg directly on top of the cheese. Cook until the edges of the egg are just set, about 2 minutes.
- Cover, lower the heat, and cook until the white is set but the yolk still runny, about 2 more minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
- Run a spatula around the edge of the egg and cheese to free them and slide onto a plate. Repeat with the remaining cheese and egg.
