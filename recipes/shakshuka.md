60 min active; ideally give yourself 2 hours before serving. For non-veg, add sausage (Jimmy Dean) or bacon to it.

**Sous vide eggs (75 min)**

- 146°F for 75 minutes.
- If no sous vide, [follow the instructions in step 2 here](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1014721-shakshuka-with-feta) (note: preheat oven).

**Prep (30 min)**

- Prep onion & bell pepper (can go in same bowl).
- Prep garlic.
- Prep spice mix: cumin, paprika, cayenne, saffron, harissa (can go in one bowl).

**Cook (30 min)**

- Oil in Dutch oven, medium. Add onion and bell pepper. Cook until soft, stirring occasionally, ~20 min.
- Add garlic, 1-2 min, stir.
- Switch to medium-low.
- Add spice mix, stir, 1 min (just toasting/mixing).
- Add tomatoes + salt & pepper. Stir occasionally, 10 min.
- Add feta, stir.
- Switch to low, simmer for however long you want (1-2 hours seems great; let the flavors condense).
- Serve with the eggs and cilantro.
