**Curry**

- On the Instant Pot's high Sauté setting, heat the ghee. Cook the onion until translucent, 4-5 minutes. Press Cancel.
- Add the chicken, tomato purée, water, ginger, garlic, chile powder, salt, garam masala and turmeric and stir.

**Rice (pot-in-pot)**

- In a 1 1/2-quart stainless bowl that fits the pot, stir the rice, water and salt. Set a tall steam rack in the pot with its legs on the bottom and the bowl of rice on top.

**Pressure cook**

- Lock the lid, valve to Sealing. Pressure Cook on high for 5 minutes.
- Natural release 5 minutes, then vent the rest. Lift out the rice bowl (mitts) and the rack.

**Finish**

- Stir in the coconut cream, tomato paste, fenugreek and sugar. High Sauté until the curry boils and is heated through, about 2 minutes. Press Cancel.
- Spoon the rice onto plates, ladle the curry over, and scatter with cilantro.

**Notes**

- Coconut cream: the unsweetened canned kind, not the sweet cocktail kind. Or chill a can of full-fat coconut milk 4-5 hours and scoop the cream off the top.
