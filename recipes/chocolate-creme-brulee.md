**Day 1**

**Macerate the blueberries**

- Put the sugar, water, star anise and lemon verbena in a pot. Heat until the sugar dissolves, then take off the heat and steep 20 minutes.
- Strain and cool. Pour over the blueberries and refrigerate overnight.

**Day 2**

**Crème brûlée**

- Heat the oven to 300°F.
- In a double boiler (water at a bare simmer, 180-190°F), heat the cream, half and half and chocolate until the chocolate is completely melted.
- In a separate bowl, whisk the egg yolks with the 1/3 cup sugar.
- Strain the chocolate cream so there are no clumps, and let it cool slightly.
- Slowly temper the chocolate cream into the yolks: a ladle at a time at first, whisking, then the rest. Keep it under 175°F so the eggs don't scramble.
- Fill ramekins with the custard. The source says 8 oz per ramekin; about 5 cups of custard fills eight 5-oz ramekins, so use whatever size you have and don't overfill.
- Set the ramekins in a deep baking dish and pour in hot water to halfway up their sides.
- Bake at 300°F for 50 minutes, until just set with a slight wobble in the center. Cool, then chill.

**Chocolate biscotti**

- Heat the oven to 350°F.
- Whip the eggs with the 3/4 cup sugar until the mixture holds ribbons.
- Mix in the flour, cocoa, baking soda, salt and chocolate chips.
- Shape into two 8x3-inch logs on a parchment-lined sheet tray.
- Bake 20 minutes. Let cool, slice into 1/2-inch pieces, lay them flat, and bake 10 minutes more.

**Assembly**

- Sprinkle each chilled custard with about a teaspoon of sugar and caramelize with a kitchen torch until the top is glassy and amber.
- Strain the blueberries from their syrup and divide them over the custards.
- Serve each with one or two pieces of biscotti.

**Notes**

- The winery pairs it with its single-vineyard Cabernet Sauvignons, Blend 26 or Blend 27.
- Nutrition counts the custards, the biscotti and the berries; the steeped syrup, star anise and verbena are strained off and mostly excluded.
