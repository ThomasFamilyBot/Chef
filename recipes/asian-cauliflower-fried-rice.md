**Rice the cauliflower**

- Pulse the florets in a food processor until the pieces look like rice or couscous. Skip this with bagged riced cauliflower; use frozen straight from the freezer (thawed goes soggy) and add a minute or two to the cook.

**Crisp the bacon**

- Put the diced bacon in a large cast-iron skillet over medium heat (pan surface about 300-350°F) and cook until the bits are crispy.
- Move the bacon to a plate with a slotted spoon; leave the fat in the pan.

**Make the omelet**

- While the bacon cooks, whisk the eggs with salt and pepper (`3 -> Omelet`).
- Pour the eggs into the hot bacon fat and cook a thin omelet, about 2 minutes on the first side and 30 seconds on the second.
- Move to a cutting board, slice thinly, and set aside.

**Cook the onion & mushrooms**

- Raise the heat to medium-high (pan surface about 375-425°F). Add the onion with a pinch of salt and pepper.
- Once the onion softens, add the mushrooms with more salt and pepper and stir-fry until their liquid has evaporated and they've browned a bit.

**Add the ginger & cauliflower**

- Add the ginger and stir about 30 seconds, until fragrant.
- Add the cauliflower and season with more salt and pepper.
- Cover, turn the heat to low, and cook about 5 minutes, until the cauliflower is tender but not mushy.

**Finish**

- Stir in the coconut aminos, herbs, sliced omelet, and a splash of vinegar if you like (`6 -> Finish`). Splash on the fish sauce.
- Taste and adjust the seasoning. Scatter the crispy bacon over the top and serve.

**Notes**

- Swap the bacon for any leftover cooked protein: roast chicken, ham, char siu. No protein? Scramble more eggs.
- 1/4 cup of Nom Nom Paleo's All-Purpose Stir-Fry Sauce can replace the coconut aminos, vinegar and fish sauce.
- Keeps 4 days in the fridge or 2 months frozen.
