Pan-fried from frozen.

**1. Pan-fry the bottoms**

- 5 tsp oil (1 2/3 TBLS) in the pan, dumplings in.
- Low (pan surface 200-250°F) for 5 min - looking for lightly toasted bottom.

**2. Steam**

- 1/2 cup broth OR water.
- Cover.
- 8 min.
