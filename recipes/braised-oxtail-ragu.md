6 hours minimum = 2 hours Active + 4 hours passive. Serve with pasta.

**Sear Oxtail**

- Oven to 250°F.
- 1/4 cup of oil into large dutch oven, high heat.
- Pat Oxtails dry, salt and pepper all over.
- Brown Oxtail on each side, 6-8 min a side. Batches if necessary. Put on platter.
- Drain excess fat, leave ~1/4 of oil in.

**Braise Oxtail**

- Heat to medium.
- Put in `Chppd -> Oxtail Base`. Let brown/wilt. 2-3 minutes.
- Add 1 TBLS of tomato paste, cook for 5-6 min.
- Add Oxtail, garlic halves, bottle of wine. Bring to boil.
    - If wine doesn't cover meat, use water (may want to try beef stock).
- Once boiling, scrape brown bits from Dutch Oven.
- Put in oven, uncovered. 3-4 hours (until falling apart tender).

**Prepare Sauce Base**

- New dutch oven on med-low. 3 TBLS of olive oil.
- `Proces -> Sauce Base` into dutch oven, saute until onions are translucent (7-8 min).
- Add 3/4 cup water (or beef stock) & 1 TBLS of tomato paste.
- Let cook until veggies are basically dissolved (liquid should reduce).
- Add San Marzano tomatoes (break up with fork or masher).
- Simmer for 15-20 min, reserve for oxtail.

**Finish Oxtail Braise**

- Take oxtail braise from oven and filter cooking liquid from solids. RESERVE liquid.
    - Remove fat that comes to top of reserved liquid.
- Let oxtail cool and shred into bite size pieces.
- Put filtered braising liquid + shredded oxtail into Sauce Base.
- Simmer until syrupy enough to coat a noodle. 20-35 min, hours if you got it.
    - Dashes of Wondra will thicken it up.
    - Decide when serving and boil pasta water accordingly.
- Probably will need more salt and pepper.
- Lasts in fridge for 4 days.
