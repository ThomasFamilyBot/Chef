**Marinade**

- Whisk the mustard, Worcestershire, garlic, 2 TBLS lemon juice and 2 tsp pepper in a medium bowl. Whisking constantly, stream in 1/3 cup oil until emulsified.

**Special sauce**

- Move half the marinade to a small bowl and whisk in the mayonnaise and cornichons. Season with salt; set aside.

**Chicken**

- Season the thighs with salt and toss in the remaining marinade. Let sit at room temperature at least 30 minutes, tossing now and then, or chill up to 4 hours.
- Heat a grill for medium-high (grate 400-450°F) and oil the grate. Grill the chicken, turning once, until well charred and cooked through (175°F), 8-10 minutes. Rest 5 minutes. With 6 small thighs, halve 2 and use 1 1/2 per sandwich.

**Buns**

- Grill the buns cut side down until lightly charred, about 30 seconds.

**Fennel salad**

- Toss the fennel fronds, sliced fennel, basil, red pepper flakes (if using), remaining 2 tsp oil and 2 tsp lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper.

**Assemble**

- Spread the special sauce on the cut sides of each bun. Lettuce and tomato on the bottoms, then chicken, then a pile of fennel salad. Close and serve.
