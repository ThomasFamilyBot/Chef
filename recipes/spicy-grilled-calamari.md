**Paprika paste**

- In a small bowl, combine the smoked paprika, cumin, salt, pepper, cayenne, garlic and the zest of the lemon. Stir in the olive oil to a smooth, runny paste.

**Finishing sauce**

- Juice the zested lemon into a second small bowl. Stir in 2 TBLS of the paprika paste. Cover and set aside.

**Marinate and grill**

- Put the remaining paste in a large zip-top bag, add the squid, and toss to coat. Seal and refrigerate 30 minutes.
- Meanwhile, soak the bamboo skewers in cold water and heat a gas or charcoal grill on high (grate 500°F+).
- Thread the tubes and tentacles onto skewers (two per skewer stops them spinning). Discard the marinade.
- Grill 2-3 minutes per side, just until opaque. Pull them off right away and drizzle with the lemon-paprika sauce.
- Garnish with lemon wedges. Serve with crusty bread for the sauce.

**Notes**

- About 20 pieces per pound, so around five per person; break it onto more skewers for an appetizer crowd.
- Nutrition counts half the oil: the marinade is discarded, the finishing sauce isn't.
