**Heat the oven**

- Set a rack in the middle of the oven. Heat the oven to 425°F.
- Rub the 1 TBLS soft butter from `2 -> Pan` over the bottom and sides of a rimmed half sheet pan (13 by 18 inches).

**Make the batter**

- Make the batter exactly as the box directs, using the whole box.
- Mix until just combined. Mochi mix clumps, so whisk out the big lumps, but don't beat it.
- Let the batter rest 5 minutes.

**Fill the pan**

- Pour the batter into the buttered pan and spread it evenly, all the way into the corners.
- If adding chocolate chips or berries, scatter them over the top now. Pat wet berries dry first.

**Bake**

- Bake 15 minutes. Start checking at 12.
- It is done when the top is golden, the edges are pulling away from the pan, the center springs back when pressed, and a toothpick comes out clean.

**Serve**

- Cool 3 to 5 minutes in the pan. Cut into 12 squares and serve with butter and maple syrup.

**Notes**

- Tested by the family on 2026-11-23: 425°F was great. Before that they had always baked it at about 350°F.
- The batter amounts in the table are what a grocery listing shows for this mix, tripled for the whole box (1 cup mix, 1/2 cup milk, 1 egg, 2 TBLS oil or butter makes 6 pancakes). The box in your hand wins.
- Mochi mix is made from sweet rice flour, so it bakes up chewy. It can look done on top while the middle is still gummy. When in doubt, give it 2 more minutes; it also firms up as it cools.

**Other ways to bake it**

- Gentler and more even: 375°F for about 20 minutes, turning the pan around halfway. Paler, softer edges. Try this if 425°F browns the edges before the center sets.
- The family's old way: about 350°F, which takes longer and browns less.
- More color: finish under the broiler for up to 1 minute, watching the whole time.
- Convection oven: 400°F, and start checking at 10 minutes.
- Half a box: use a quarter sheet pan or a 9-by-13-inch pan, same temperature, and start checking at 10 minutes.
- A whole box in a 9-by-13-inch pan makes a thick, cakey pancake: 375°F for about 22 to 25 minutes (estimate).

**With a regular buttermilk pancake mix**

- Use about 3 cups of mix, prepared as its own box directs. One tested version uses 3 cups mix, 2 cups milk, 2 eggs and 1/4 cup melted butter.
- Bake at 425°F for 12 to 14 minutes. Wheat-based batter rises higher and sets faster than mochi batter, so check early.
- A "complete" mix that only needs water takes longer: a 1 lb bag with 2 cups water bakes 15 to 20 minutes at 425°F.

**Leftovers**

- Keep 4 to 5 days in the fridge, or freeze the squares. Reheat in the toaster or microwave.
