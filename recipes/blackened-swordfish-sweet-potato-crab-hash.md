**BBQ sauce** (restaurant batch; makes about 6 quarts)

- In a large pot, sweat the butter, onion and garlic (200-250°F, no color) until translucent.
- In a large bowl, blend the vinegar, apricot jam, dry mustard, brown sugar and Dijon until smooth.
- Add to the pot with everything else in `1 -> BBQ sauce`. Bring to a simmer (185-205°F) and cook 1 hour. Freeze what you don't use.

**Voodoo sauce** (makes about 3 cups)

- In a saucepan over low heat, reduce the cream by a third.
- In another saucepan, heat 2 cups of the BBQ sauce. Add the reduced cream, then the butter, and simmer 3-4 minutes. Whisk in the salt and pepper. Keep warm.

**Sweet potato crab hash** (makes 2 servings)

- Heat the olive oil in a sauté pan over medium-high (pan surface 375-425°F). Add the roasted sweet potato, shallot and poblano and sauté 2 minutes.
- Add 3 oz of the Voodoo sauce, the Worcestershire, and salt and pepper to taste. Add the crab and toss gently just until heated through.

**Swordfish**

- Dredge both sides of the steak in the blackening spice.
- Heat a heavy cast-iron skillet over high heat until smoking hot (500°F+). Cook the swordfish 3 minutes, turn, and cook 3 minutes more (130-135°F internal for just-cooked).

**Plate**

- Spoon 4 oz of warm Voodoo sauce onto the plate, top with 2 cups of the hash, then the swordfish. Garnish with the tomato and scallions.

**Notes**

- A restaurant recipe, untested for home use. The BBQ sauce batch is enormous (a #10 can of chili sauce); make it once and freeze it, or buy a good BBQ sauce and start at the Voodoo sauce with 2 cups of it.
- Nutrition counts only the portion of each sauce that reaches the plate: about 2.5% of the BBQ sauce ingredients and 30% of the Voodoo sauce. The hash is counted in full even though it makes 2 servings.
