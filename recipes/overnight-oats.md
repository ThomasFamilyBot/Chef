**Oat base**

- In a Mason jar or other lidded jar, combine the oats, chia seeds, 1/2 tsp maple syrup, salt and the yogurt, if using.
- Pour in the almond milk and stir until well mixed with no clumps of chia at the bottom. Cover and refrigerate overnight, or up to 5 days.

**Toppings**

- In the morning, add toppings and a drizzle of maple syrup.

**Variations** (per jar)

- *Apple pie*: stir 2 TBLS unsweetened applesauce and 1/4 tsp cinnamon (or apple pie spice) into the base before chilling. Top with diced apple, chopped pecans and warm cinnamon apples (the library has a recipe under Sides).
- *Peach crisp*: plain base; top with peach slices and granola.
- *PB&J*: plain base; top with chia jam, peanut butter, chopped strawberries, raspberries and chopped peanuts. (Or almond butter and toasted almonds.)
- *Chocolate banana bread*: stir 1/2 mashed banana, 1 tsp cocoa powder, 1/4 tsp cinnamon and a pinch of nutmeg into the base. Top with banana slices, chopped walnuts and chocolate chips.
- *Chia jam*: cook 1 lb strawberries with 1/2 tsp lemon juice, 1 tsp maple syrup and a pinch of salt over low heat 3-5 minutes until soft; mash, leaving some chunks. Off the heat stir in 3 TBLS chia seeds. Cool uncovered, then chill.

**Notes**

- The ratio: 1/2 cup oats to 2/3 cup milk to 1 TBLS chia; add 1/4 cup Greek yogurt for extra-creamy, higher-protein oats.
- Meal prep: line up a jar per serving with everything but the milk, then add and stir the milk one jar at a time so the chia doesn't clump.
- One jar is about 1 1/2 cups. Nutrition is the base only (with yogurt); toppings aren't counted.
