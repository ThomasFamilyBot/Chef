45 min to get to beans step 2 (prep + beans step 1). 90 min for beans. 30 min for lamb. 2 hrs in oven. ~5 hours. Could save ~30 min by starting lamb towards the end of beans step 2.

**Prep (can start Beans step 1 while doing prep)**

*----> can combine chopped thyme, chopped parsley and tomatoes*

- Beans - Pick & Rinse
- Onions - 2 onions finely chopped
- Garlic - 15 minced (~7 TBLS)

*---> can combine: chopped onion and minced garlic*

- Onion - 1 peel and halve w/ root end intact - stick 1 clove into each half
- Garlic - 5 whole
- Carrot - Peeled & halved
- Celery -> Halved
- Thyme Sprig
- Bay Leaf
- Salt Pork - 3 equal pieces

*---> can combine: 7 1/2 cups water + both onion / clove halves + whole garlic + carrot + celery + thyme sprig + bay leaf + salt pork*

- Lamb -> 1" cubes
- Parsley -> finely chopped

**Cook**

*Beans - Step 1 (~25 min)*

- Large Pot - beans + enough cold water to cover by 1".
- Bring to a boil.
- Remove from heat for 15 min (stir occasionally).
- Drain water, return beans to pot.

*Beans - Step 2 (~90 min)*

- Beans should be in pot already.
- 7 1/2 cups water to pot + both onion / clove halves + whole garlic + carrot + celery + thyme sprig + bay leaf + salt pork.
- Bring to a boil, then reduce to low simmer.
- Stir occasionally until beans are tender but just whole (~1 hour).
- ~5 min before finished, stir in 2 TBLS of salt.
- Remove from heat and let rest for 15 min.
- Drain -> reserve cooking liquid and beans. Pick out everything that's easy to get.

*Lamb - Brown (~30 min)*

- Large Pot (can be bean pot, just wipe it clean first), heat 4 TBLS (= 2 fl oz) of olive oil over medium high.
- Work in batches to brown lamb. Salt the side you are about to cook. Get a deep brown on at least 2 sides (~4 min per side). Transfer to plate when done.

*Make the liquid base (~15 min)*

- Reduce heat to med-low on stove.
- Preheat oven to 350°F (for next step).
- Stir in chopped onions + minced garlic. Slow cook until onions are pale gold at the edges (~10 min).
- + wine and simmer for 3 min. Scrape bottom of pot to get browned bits.
- + tomatoes, chopped thyme, parsley, 3 cups of bean liquid (if need more liquid, chicken broth or water).
- + A few grinds of pepper. Bring to low simmer (start working on next step while waiting).

*Casserole - Step 1 (30 min)*

- 1 TBLS of olive oil into 8x12 oven dish.
- Spread half of the beans, distribute lamb, pour in remaining beans.
- Pour in bean liquid.
- Sprinkle bread crumbs.
- Drizzle remaining olive oil (3 TBLS or 1 1/2 fl oz).
- Bake for 30 min.

*Casserole - Step 2 (~2 hr)*

- Reduce heat to 325°F and continue baking until lamb is tender and liquid covers ~25% of the dish. ~1.5 hours.
- Adjust oven temp to maintain bare simmer and don't get fooled by bubbling liquid (might be less liquid than you think).
- Preheat broiler and crisp the top. Likely < 5 min, can take as long as 15.
- Truffle salt for topping.
