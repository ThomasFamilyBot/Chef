**Bread and chill the goat cheese**

- Slice the log into 1 oz rounds and pat each one firmly together with your hands.
- Set up three bowls: flour; egg beaten with a dash of water; bread crumbs. Dip each round in flour, then egg, then crumbs, shaking off the excess. Season with salt and pepper.
- Set on a clean plate and refrigerate at least 1 hour (or freeze 15 minutes). Cold rounds hold together in the pan.

**Make the strawberry dressing**

- Blend everything in `2 -> Strawberry dressing` until creamy. Keep chilled until serving.

**Fry the goat cheese**

- Heat a small cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat (pan surface 375-425°F). Add the olive oil and let it heat briefly.
- Add the cold breaded rounds. Watch closely and flip once the first side is nicely browned; keep going until all sides are browned, holding the rounds on edge with tongs to color the sides.

**Assemble**

- Divide the greens, radishes, red onion, almonds, berries and chicken among four bowls.
- Set the warm goat cheese on top and drizzle with the strawberry dressing.

**Notes**

- Best eaten warm. Reheat leftover rounds briefly in the microwave or a skillet.
