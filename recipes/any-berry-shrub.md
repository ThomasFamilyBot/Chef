**Day 1: macerate**

- Gently mash the berries in a medium bowl with a fork. Move to a 1-quart glass jar, add the sugar, and stir.
- Seal and leave at room temperature, shaking a couple of times, until the berries are very soft and falling apart and the sugar is mostly dissolved, about 1 day. It should look very juicy.

**Day 2: shrub**

- Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a medium bowl (line it with cheesecloth to catch every seed). Save the fruit for pancakes or a smoothie, or discard. Scrape any undissolved sugar from the jar into the bowl.
- Stir in the vinegar. Taste: it should be both sweet and sharp without either taking over, remembering it mellows as it ages. Adjust with more sugar or vinegar.
- Pour into a clean jar, cover, and chill until the flavors come together, about 1 week.

**Serve**

- Pour 2 TBLS shrub over ice in a glass and top with club soda; stir gently. Garnish with berries and/or an herb sprig.

**Notes**

- Keeps 6 months in the fridge; it's smoother after the first week.
- Good with gin, vodka or tequila too: 2 TBLS shrub, 1 1/2 fl oz spirit, soda.
- Nutrition is the shrub only (whole batch, about 20 drinks); the club soda and garnish aren't counted. The strained fruit pulp is counted as if consumed, so it's a slight overestimate.
