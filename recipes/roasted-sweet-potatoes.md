**Roast**

- Set a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 450°F.
- Toss everything in `1 -> Roast` on a rimmed baking sheet.
- Roast, tossing occasionally, until tender and browned, 35-45 minutes.

**Variations (toss with the oil, salt and pepper before roasting unless noted)**

- Spiced: 2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika.
- Cinnamon-sugar: 1/4 cup packed dark brown sugar and 1 tsp ground cinnamon.
- Herby Parmesan: after roasting, sprinkle with finely grated Parmesan and chopped tender herbs (dill or parsley).
- Glazed: after roasting, drizzle with maple syrup, agave, honey or pomegranate molasses.

**Do ahead**

- Roast up to 3 days ahead. Cool, then chill in an airtight container.
