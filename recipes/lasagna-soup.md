**Brown the meat**

- Heat a large soup pot (7-quart Dutch oven) over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F). Add the sausage, ground beef and olive oil and cook until browned, about 5 minutes.

**Soup**

- Add the marinara, broth, onion, garlic, tomato paste, salt, dried parsley, pepper, oregano and dried basil. Bring to a boil.
- Cover, turn to low, and simmer (185-205°F) about 20 minutes.

**Cheese topping**

- Meanwhile, mix the ricotta, mozzarella, Parmesan and 2 TBLS of the fresh parsley in a bowl. Set aside for serving.

**Noodles**

- Soak the lasagna noodles in a pan of water deep enough to submerge them, stirring gently so they don't stick, 3-4 minutes. Lift out and cut into pieces on a board. Soaking pulls off starch and makes them easy to cut.
- Add the noodles to the pot and cook at a low boil to your liking, 5-10 minutes; the last 5 minutes with the lid off.

**Serve**

- Ladle into bowls, top with a dollop of the cheese mixture, the remaining 1 TBLS parsley and fresh basil if you like.

**Notes**

- Best the day it's made; the noodles soak up broth as it sits. For leftovers, cook and store the pasta separately and add broth when reheating. Keeps 3 days.
- Ground turkey or chicken works in place of the beef and sausage; all beef or all sausage is fine too.
