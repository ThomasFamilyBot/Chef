**Heat the oil**

- In a deep fryer or saucepan, heat 4 inches of oil to 375°F.

**Batter**

- In a large bowl, whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, 1 tsp salt and the baking powder.
- In another bowl, whisk the milk and egg. Stir the wet into the dry until just combined.

**Fry**

- Coat the hot dog pieces evenly in batter.
- Working in batches, fry until golden brown all over, turning if needed, about 2 minutes. Keep the oil near 375°F between batches.
- Drain on paper towels and season with kosher salt while warm.

**Notes**

- Whole hot dogs on sticks work too, for the full fair effect.
- NYT's own estimate: 352 calories per serving.
