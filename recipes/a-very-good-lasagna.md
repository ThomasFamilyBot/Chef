**Make the sauce**

- Heat the olive oil in a large, heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat (pan surface about 300-350°F).
- Add the onion, garlic and anchovies; season with salt and pepper. Sweat, stirring occasionally, until the onion is completely soft and translucent without browning, 8-10 minutes.
- Add the tomato paste, if using, and cook, stirring, until it turns a deeper brick red, about 2 minutes.
- Add the hand-crushed whole tomatoes and the crushed tomatoes, scraping up any bits from the bottom. Fill one tomato can halfway with water, swirl, and add it. Season with salt and pepper.
- Bring to a simmer (185-205°F) and cook, stirring occasionally, until thickened and the flavors come together, 35-45 minutes. It should be as thick as jarred tomato sauce; looser and the lasagna will be too wet to cut cleanly.

**Make the cheese mixture**

- Heat the oven to 425°F and bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
- Set aside 1 cup of the mozzarella for the top. In a medium bowl, combine the rest of `2 -> Cheese mixture` (mozzarella, ricotta, Parmesan, cream, grated garlic); season with salt and pepper.

**Cook the noodles**

- Boil the noodles until just softened, before al dente, 4-5 minutes.
- Drain, separate any that are sticking together, and drizzle with a little olive oil.

**Assemble**

- Spoon a little sauce over the bottom of a 3-quart baking dish and add a layer of noodles, avoiding heavy overlap (some is fine).
- Top with about 1 1/4 cups sauce and dollop one-fourth of the cheese mixture over it.
- Repeat three more times, ending with the last noodles and the last of the sauce. Depending on the dish, you may have a few noodles left over.
- Top with the reserved 1 cup mozzarella and more Parmesan, if you like.

**Bake twice**

- Cover loosely with foil and set the dish on a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet to catch drips.
- Bake covered until the pasta is completely tender and the sauce bubbles around the edges, 25-30 minutes. This short first bake only melts the cheese and heats the sauce through.
- Uncover and bake until golden brown on top with frilly, crispy edges and corners, another 35-45 minutes. When you think it's done, consider going a little longer; browner is better.
- Let cool slightly before cutting.

**Do ahead**

- Sauce: up to 1 week ahead, covered and refrigerated.
- Baked lasagna: up to 3 days, wrapped tightly and refrigerated, or 1 month frozen (thaw first). Reheat covered with foil at 375°F for 25-45 minutes.
- Shortcut: skip the sauce and use two jars of good tomato-garlic sauce.
