**Make the chocolate base**

- In a saucepan, whisk 1 cup of the cream with the cocoa from `1 -> Chocolate base`. Bring to a boil (212°F), then lower the heat and simmer 30 seconds, whisking the whole time.
- Off the heat, add both chopped chocolates and whisk until completely smooth, with no flecks left. Whisk in the remaining 1 cup cream.
- Pour into a large bowl and set a fine mesh strainer over it.

**Temper the yolks**

- Whisk the yolks from `2 -> Yolks` in a small bowl.
- In the saucepan over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F), whisk together everything from `3 -> Custard`. When the milk is just warm, whisk 1/2 cup of it into the yolks, then pour the yolk mixture back into the pan, whisking as it goes in.

**Cook the custard**

- Keep cooking over medium heat, stirring constantly with a spatula, until it thickens and lightly coats the spatula, 170°F. Don't go past 180°F or the eggs scramble.
- Pour the custard through the strainer into the chocolate base. Stir until combined; discard what's left in the strainer.
- Stir in the vanilla from `4 -> Finish`.

**Chill**

- Set the bowl in an ice bath and stir often until cold, then cover and refrigerate until fully chilled, at least 6 hours. The source says overnight is fine.
- House note from Chocolate & Peeps Ice Cream: a chocolate base held in the fridge can turn grainy. If the chilled base looks thick or flecked, whisk it hard (or use an immersion blender) until smooth before churning.

**Churn**

- Start the ice cream maker first, then pour in the cold base (it locks the dasher if poured into a still bowl). Churn until thick, like soft serve, 30-40 minutes in the source's machine; the KitchenAid bowl usually gets there in 20-30.
- Pack into an airtight container and freeze at least 6 hours.

**Serve**

- No stabilizers, so it freezes hard. Leave it out 5-10 minutes before scooping.
- Keeps in the freezer up to 2 months.

**Adult version**

- Follow the original recipe (link in the Reference line): 6 oz of 60% dark chocolate in place of the semisweet and milk chocolate, and natural unsweetened cocoa powder. Everything else is the same.
