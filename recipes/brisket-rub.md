**Rub**

- Mix the mustard powder, salt, paprika, cinnamon, pepper, garlic powder and brown sugar in a bowl until fully combined.
- Use right away, or store airtight in a cool, dark place up to 4 weeks.

**Notes**

- A healthy amount of brown sugar builds the bark: the dark crust that forms during the hotter, later part of a brisket cook.
- Nutrition is the whole batch (two briskets' worth); a brisket only picks up part of what you sprinkle on.
