When combining ingredients, you want them all at room temperature. Take the milk & eggs out before starting the recipe and let the butter cool a bit from being melted before you start combining ingredients.

**Batter**

- Preheat oven to 450°F. Put the ramekins in the oven so they heat up too.
- Mix eggs together, then add to the milk. Mix together.
- Add 2 TBLS + 1 tsp salt.
- Gently pour the egg mixture into the flour. Don't over-mix.
- Once it's just mixed in (a few clumps okay), add 10 TBLS (1 stick + 2 TBLS) of melted / clarified butter.
- Move quickly to get the popover batter into the ramekins.
- Brush the inside of the ramekins with butter using a pastry brush.
- Fill popover molds with batter.

**Baking**

- 20 minutes at 450°F.
- 10 minutes at 350°F.
- 20 minutes at 325°F.

**Honey butter**

- Mix 5 oz of butter (1 1/4 sticks) with ~2 1/2 tsp honey and a pinch of salt.

**Notes**

- Thanksgiving 26 version. What changed from the original: everything is doubled, for 24 popovers. The method and the oven steps are the same.
- Flour and milk are by weight, as in the original. The flour weight assumes 125 g per cup.
- Twenty-four popovers need 24 ramekins or two 12-cup pans, and the oven space to hold them. With one pan, bake in two rounds of 12: keep the rest of the batter at room temperature, and bring the oven and the empty pan back to 450°F before the second round. The second round adds another 50 minutes.
- The oven steps run 450°F, 350°F, 325°F, so the popovers can't share the oven with the 350°F dishes for the first 20 minutes.
