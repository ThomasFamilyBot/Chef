**Garlic & oil**

- In a large saucepan, heat the olive oil over medium heat (pan surface about 300-350°F).
- Add the garlic and red pepper flakes and sauté until golden, about a minute.

**Sauce**

- Add the salt, pepper, wine and butter.
- Stir 2 minutes, then take off the heat.
- Toss with pasta, chicken, scallops or shrimp.

**Pasta (optional)**

- Bring a pot of salted water to a boil (212°F) and cook the pasta al dente.
- Toss with the sauce and garnish with parsley and Parmesan.

**Notes**

- The source's notes were partly hidden in the printout; what was legible: salted or unsalted butter both work, and a dry white such as chardonnay is the wine to use.
- Nutrition is for the sauce only; the optional pasta and garnishes are not counted.
