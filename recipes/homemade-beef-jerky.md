**Day 1**

**Slice the beef**

- Freeze the roast 1-2 hours first; it slices much more easily.
- Slice between 1/8 and 1/4 inch thick, with the grain. If the roast is too thick to slice comfortably, halve it horizontally first.

**Marinade**

- In a medium bowl, whisk the brown sugar, soy sauce, Worcestershire, smoked paprika, meat tenderizer, black pepper, red pepper flakes, onion powder and garlic powder until the sugar dissolves.
- Add the meat and toss until every piece is coated. Cover (or bag it) and refrigerate at least 12 hours or overnight, tossing once or twice.

**Day 2**

**Dry in the oven**

- Line two baking sheets with foil and set a wire rack on each. Heat the oven to 175°F with two racks in the center positions.
- Lay the marinated slices on the racks in a single layer.
- Bake 3-4 hours, swapping the pans front to back and top to bottom halfway, until dried out. To check, cool a piece to room temperature: it should be dry to the touch, leathery, and chewy but still a little tender.

**Store**

- Cool completely. Keeps about a week at room temperature in an airtight container, 3 weeks refrigerated, or 6 months frozen.

**Notes**

- Eye of round is ideal: lean, cheap and easy to trim.
- Meat tenderizer contains bromelain, which breaks down the muscle; it's in the spice aisle.
- Nutrition counts 15% of the marinade, since the rest is drained off.
