**Roast the garlic**

- Heat the oven to 350°F. Slice 1/4 inch off the top of the garlic head.
- Set it cut-side up on a piece of foil (a ramekin helps it sit upright), drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with salt, and wrap.
- Roast until the cloves are deeply golden brown and tender, 40-60 minutes; keep going if they aren't there yet. Squeeze out 7 cloves for the mash. ([Love and Lemons roasted garlic](https://www.loveandlemons.com/roasted-garlic-recipe/))
- Already have roasted garlic? Skip this step and the whole recipe takes about 20 minutes.

**Boil the cauliflower**

- Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil (212°F).
- Add the cauliflower and boil until knife-tender, about 10 minutes. Drain and move to a food processor.

**Puree**

- Puree the cauliflower with the butter, the 7 roasted garlic cloves, mustard, sea salt and pepper from `3 -> Puree` until smooth. Work in batches if the processor is small.
- Season to taste and garnish with chives, if using.

**Notes**

- Scales up easily for a crowd.
- Keeps 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in the microwave or in a covered baking dish at 350°F.
- Leftover roasted cloves freeze, peeled and airtight, up to 2 months.
