**Crunch** (the part everyone talks about)

- Pulse 14 oz of the Oreos in a food processor to coarse crumbs: pea- to marble-size pieces with some dust, not powder.
- Spread on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Drizzle the whole bottle of Magic Shell over and toss with a fork until every crumb is coated but the mixture is still loose and craggy.
- Freeze 20 minutes, until hard. Break into small clusters with your hands. Keep frozen; set aside about 1 cup for the top.

**Crust**

- Line a 9-inch springform with a parchment round on the bottom and a 3-inch parchment strip around the side.
- Process the remaining 8 oz Oreos to fine crumbs, mix with the melted butter, and press firmly into the bottom of the pan. Freeze 15 minutes.

**First layer**

- Spread the vanilla ice cream over the crust in an even layer; an offset spatula dipped in hot water helps. Freeze 30-45 minutes, until firm to the touch.

**Fudge and crunch**

- Spread the warm (not hot) fudge thinly over the vanilla layer. Scatter all but the reserved cup of crunch over it in a thick, even layer and press gently so it anchors. Freeze 15 minutes.

**Second layer**

- Spread the chocolate ice cream over the crunch and smooth the top. Cover and freeze at least 4 hours, ideally overnight.

**Whipped cream**

- Whip the cream, sugar, vanilla and pudding mix to firm peaks.
- Unmold: wrap a warm, damp towel around the ring for 20 seconds, release the spring, and peel off the parchment. Frost the top and sides with the cream. Press the reserved crunch over the top (or around the edge).
- Freeze 1 hour to set the cream. Rest 10 minutes at room temperature, then slice with a long knife dipped in hot water and wiped between cuts.

**Notes**

- Flavor pairs: vanilla cake = Golden Oreos + vanilla (white) Magic Shell; chocolate cake = chocolate or regular Oreos + chocolate Magic Shell. Mint chip + chocolate crunch and coffee + Golden crunch are both good.
- Why not Rice Krispies? They add air but no flavor, and they soften against ice cream within a day. The Oreo crumb carries cocoa and cream flavor and stays crisp for a week. If you want extra lift, toss 1 cup Rice Krispies in with the crumbs before the Magic Shell goes on.
- Homemade Magic Shell: melt 8 oz chopped chocolate (or white chocolate) with 1/3 cup refined coconut oil; use while warm and pourable.
- Keeps 1 week in the freezer, wrapped once the cream is set.
- Nutrition counts everything, including the whole bottle of Magic Shell, as eaten.
