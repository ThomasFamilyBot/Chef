**Prep the kale**

- Heat the oven to 300°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment.
- Cut the leaves off the thick stems with a knife or kitchen shears and tear into bite-size pieces.
- Wash, then dry thoroughly in a salad spinner. Wet leaves steam instead of crisping.

**Bake**

- Drizzle the kale with the olive oil and toss to coat. Spread in a single layer on the sheet without overlapping, and sprinkle with the salt.
- Bake until the edges start to brown but aren't burnt, 20-30 minutes.
