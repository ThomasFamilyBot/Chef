**Day 1: rub**

- Crush the fennel seeds and peppercorns under a heavy skillet or in a mortar. Mix with 1/4 cup salt and the sugar.
- Rub all over the shoulder. Put in a large glass baking dish, cover, and refrigerate at least 6 hours, preferably overnight.

**Day 2: roast**

- Heat the oven to 325°F. Brush the rub off the meat and set the pork in a deep roasting pan; discard the excess rub and any liquid.
- Add 1 cup boiling water and cover the pan tightly with foil to trap the steam.
- Roast until very tender and falling off the bone, 195°F in the center away from the bone, about 5 1/2 hours.

**Glaze**

- Remove the foil and raise the oven to 500°F. Stir 1/4 cup vinegar and the brown sugar into the pan juices.
- Roast, watching closely, until the pork is browned and the liquid starts to reduce, about 12 minutes.

**Finish the glaze**

- Move the pork to a rimmed baking sheet. Pour the pan liquid into a small saucepan and boil, skimming fat, until thick and syrupy, about 10 minutes (about 1 1/2 cups glaze; discard up to 1 cup fat). Season with salt and pepper.

**Salad and serve**

- Toss the carrot ribbons, apple, greens, olive oil and remaining 1 TBLS vinegar; season with salt and pepper. Arrange on a platter.
- Set the pork on top, pour the glaze over, and serve with tongs.

**Notes**

- Nutrition counts 10% of the rub (it's brushed off) and the whole glaze; the discarded fat isn't subtracted.
