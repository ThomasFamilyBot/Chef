**Cube the pork belly**

- Trim off the skin and the top layer of pure fat. Cut the meat into 1 1/2-inch cubes.

**Rub**

- Drizzle the cubes with the olive oil. Mix the remaining `2 -> Rub` ingredients and sprinkle generously over the pork, rubbing it into every piece.

**Smoke**

- Heat the smoker to 225-250°F (cherry wood is the author's pick; hickory, pecan, maple, apple or peach also work).
- Arrange the cubes on a wire rack and smoke 2 1/2-3 hours, until dark red with a bark starting to form.

**Sauce**

- Move the cubes to a disposable aluminum pan, add the BBQ sauce, butter and honey, and stir to coat.
- Cover with foil and return to the smoker 60-90 minutes, until a cube probes 200-205°F in the middle, or a toothpick slides in and out easily.
- Uncover and cook 15 minutes more so the sauce thickens. Serve.

**Notes**

- Serve on toothpicks as an appetizer, or in tacos, salads, sandwiches or over nachos.
- Leftovers keep 3-4 days; reheat at 350°F for 15 minutes.
