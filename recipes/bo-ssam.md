**Day 1 — Brine the pork**

- Place pork in a large shallow bowl or roasting vessel.
- Do NOT mix the white sugar and salt. Different sized grains can mean you'll over-salt.
- Rub equal parts white sugar and salt all over the pork (suggest a handful at a time).
- Cover meat with plastic wrap, put in fridge for at least 6 hours or overnight.
    - There'll be liquid coming off, so make sure to have something that will catch it. You'll be fine if you leave it in the bowl.

**Day 2**

Less than 1 hour active time to do all the prep, but you've got to keep an eye on the pork for 6 hours.

**Cook the pork**

- Heat oven to 300°F (regular roast; convection pulls moisture).
- Remove pork from fridge, brush excess sugar off the fat cap. Discard any juices.
- Place pork in roasting pan and cook for ~6 hours.
    - Baste hourly with pan juices.
    - Looking to get most of it to 180°F; parts as low as 165°F are fine. Ideal internal temp is 190°F, but we're going to finish it under the broiler.
- OK to rest pork at room temperature for an hour.

**Make the ginger scallion sauce**

- Combine all ingredients from `1 -> Ginger Scallion Sauce` in a bowl. Suggest a food processor or a very fine chop. It's less of a sauce and more of a ginger-scallion topping.
- Let rest for at least 15 minutes. It'll keep for days.

**Make the ssam sauce**

- Combine all ingredients from `2 -> Ssam Sauce` in a bowl. Mix well.

**Prep accompaniments and put into serving dishes**

- Make the rice.
- Separate, clean, dry the lettuce.
- Shuck oysters if you're doing it.
- Kimchi.

**Finish the pork**

- Oven to 500°F.
- Stir 1 TBLS of salt and 7 TBLS of brown sugar together.
- Rub the salt-sugar mixture all over the cooked pork.
- Put in the 500°F oven until a dark caramel crust has developed, 10-15 min.
- Serve hot.
