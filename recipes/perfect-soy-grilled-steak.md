**Marinade**

- Start a charcoal or wood fire, or heat a gas grill. The fire should be hot (grate 500°F+) and the rack no more than 4 inches from the heat.
- Mix the soy sauce, ginger, garlic, honey, pepper and lime juice (`1 -> Marinade`). Taste and add more of anything you like.
- Turn the steak in the sauce once or twice, then let it sit in the sauce while the grill heats.

**Grill**

- Turn the steak once more, set it on the grill, and spoon any remaining sauce over it.
- For rare, about 3 minutes a side for steaks under an inch thick (pull at 120-125°F internal); go a little longer for thicker or more-done steak (130-135°F for medium-rare).
- Rest a few minutes before slicing.

**Notes**

- A marinade only needs to add flavor and help browning, so the soak while the grill heats is enough.
- The same sauce works on burgers, boneless chicken, tuna and swordfish. Bone-in chicken: baste with it only in the last 10 minutes.
