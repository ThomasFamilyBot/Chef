When combining ingredients, you want them all at room temperature. Take the milk & eggs out before starting the recipe and let the butter cool a bit from being melted before you start combining ingredients.

**Batter**

- Preheat oven to 450°F. Put the ramekins in the oven so they heat up too.
- Mix eggs together, then add to the milk. Mix together.
- Add 3 1/2 tsp salt.
- Gently pour the egg mixture into the flour. Don't over-mix.
- Once it's just mixed in (a few clumps okay), add 5 TBLS of melted / clarified butter.
- Move quickly to get the popover batter into the ramekins.
- Brush the inside of the ramekins with butter using a pastry brush.
- Fill popover molds with batter.

**Baking**

- 20 minutes at 450°F.
- 10 minutes at 350°F.
- 20 minutes at 325°F.

**Honey butter**

- Mix 5 TBLS of butter with ~1 1/4 tsp honey and a pinch of salt.

**Notes**

- Scaled on 2026-11-22 from the family's original 10 popovers to 12, to fill a 12-cup pan. The original amounts were 3 cups flour, 3 cups milk, 6 eggs, 1 TBLS salt and 6 oz butter, with 2 oz butter and ~1 tsp honey for the honey butter.
- Flour and milk are given by weight because 12 popovers need 3.6 cups of each, which no measuring cup hits. The flour weight assumes 125 g per cup; if you scoop a heavier cup, 3 2/3 cups by volume keeps the family's feel.
- Seven eggs stand in for the exact 7.2. Popover batter is forgiving about that.
