**Marinade**

- Heat a dry skillet over medium-high heat (pan surface 375-425°F). Toast the guajillo chiles, turning now and then, until blistered in spots and fragrant, about 3 minutes.
- Add enough water to cover and simmer 5 minutes, until softened.
- Move the chiles to a blender with everything else in `1 -> Marinade` and blend until smooth. Set aside 1/3 cup in a separate container for basting ("reserved basting sauce").

**Marinate the chicken**

- Put the chicken in a shallow dish or freezer bag, add the rest of the marinade, and stir so every piece is coated. Cover and refrigerate 8-24 hours. Refrigerate the reserved basting sauce separately.

**Skewer**

- Using 2 skewers per kabob, thread on a pineapple half-ring and push it down. Thread on about 3 thigh halves, then another pineapple half-ring, and repeat, alternating and finishing with pineapple. Make a second and third kabob until the chicken is used up.

**Grill**

- Heat the grill to medium-high (grate 400-450°F). Clean the grates and grease them generously with oil.
- Grill the kabobs 4-5 minutes without moving. Flip, brush with reserved basting sauce (never the marinade that touched raw chicken), and grill 4-5 minutes more.
- Flip again, baste, close the lid, and adjust the heat to hold about 400°F. Keep cooking, flipping and basting every 5 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165°F (175°F is better for thighs), about 10-15 minutes more. Rest 5 minutes.

**Or bake**

- Heat the oven to 400°F. Line a pan with foil and rest the metal skewers on its rim so the chicken hangs in the middle without touching the bottom.
- Bake 30 minutes, flip, brush with reserved basting sauce, and bake 15-20 minutes more, to 165°F.
- Without moving the pan, broil 3-5 minutes per side for crispy edges. Baste with the pan juices and rest 5-10 minutes.

**Carve**

- Stand the skewers upright on a cutting board and slice the chicken and pineapple off thinly. Chop finer if you like.

**Tacos**

- Serve in warm tortillas with chopped pineapple and toppings: pico de gallo, guacamole, sour cream, cotija, chipotle sauce, hot sauce and lime.

**Notes**

- Use boneless skinless thighs, not breasts: the extra fat keeps them juicy at the high heat that builds the caramelized crust.
- Achiote paste substitute (less color): add 1 1/2 TBLS paprika, 2 tsp white vinegar, 1/2 tsp dried oregano, 1/4 tsp each garlic powder, black pepper, ground cumin and cinnamon, and 1/8 tsp ground cloves, on top of the marinade spices.
- To seed guajillos: snip off the stems with kitchen shears, open each chile like a book and scrape out the seeds; wash your hands right after.
- Make ahead: the marinade keeps 5 days blended; the chicken can be cut 3 days ahead and marinated up to 24 hours. The marinade is thick, so you can thread the kabobs and let them keep marinating. Cooked, it keeps 5 days in the fridge; the chicken (not the pineapple) freezes 3 months.
- Reheat sliced chicken in a skillet with a drizzle of oil over medium heat, or microwave 30 seconds then in 15-second bursts.
- Nutrition counts the chicken, pineapple and all of the marinade (it's thick and clings, and the reserve is basted on); taco fixings aren't counted.
