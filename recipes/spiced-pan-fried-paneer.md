**Spiced oil**

- Cut the paneer into slices 1/4-1/2 inch thick, then into 1-inch pieces. Put them in a mixing bowl.
- In a small bowl, mix 1 TBLS of the oil with the turmeric, coriander, cumin, paprika and salt. Drizzle over the paneer and toss to coat; rub the spices in with your hands if you need to.

**Cook the paneer**

- Heat the remaining 1 1/2 TBLS oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F). Add the paneer and cook 3-4 minutes, until golden brown underneath.
- Flip and cook 2-3 minutes more, until golden on the other side. Move to a plate.

**Serve**

- Dust with paprika and sprinkle with chopped chives and a small pinch of flaky salt, if you like. Serve right away, with basmati or coconut rice.

**Notes**

- Best eaten immediately; reheat leftovers by crisping them again in a skillet. Doubles easily.
- Fresh paneer works too: make sure it's fully chilled so it's easier to cut, and use a nonstick pan.
- USDA has no paneer record, so nutrition uses queso blanco, the closest fresh, non-melting white cheese.
- Source's own estimate: 157 calories per quarter of the recipe.
