**Achiote garlic oil**

- Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-low heat (pan surface 250-300°F). Add the garlic, cumin, salt, pepper, oregano and achiote paste (if using), breaking up and smashing the paste as best you can. Cook several minutes, stirring.
- Take off the heat and let cool.

**Marinade**

- In a bowl, whisk the cooled achiote garlic oil with the orange and lime juice.
- Set aside 1/4 cup in the bowl for basting.

**Marinate the chicken**

- Pour the rest of the marinade into a 1- or 2-gallon zip-top freezer bag, add the chicken, seal, and massage to coat.
- Refrigerate 1 hour, 4 hours at most; any longer and the citrus breaks down the chicken and turns it mushy.

**Grill**

- Set up the grill two-zone, one hot side and one cool side, at 350-400°F: on charcoal, bank the lit coals on one half and leave the other half empty; on gas, light only half the burners (Kevin keeps top and bottom on medium, middle on low).
- Lift the chicken out of the bag and discard the bag of marinade.
- Grill the chicken covered, on the cool side, until an instant-read thermometer reads 165°F in the thickest part of each piece (175°F is better for thighs and legs), 20-30 minutes. Baste with the reserved marinade every few minutes. No need to turn the pieces; you're not after grill marks.
- Discard any leftover basting marinade.

**Notes**

- Achiote paste usually comes in 4 oz blocks; this uses half. Save the rest for another batch, or for the [Chicken Al Pastor](https://thomasfamilybot.github.io/Chef/r/chicken-al-pastor.html).

- Nutrition counts the whole chicken at the table's edible yield and about a quarter of the marinade (the basting reserve plus what clings).
