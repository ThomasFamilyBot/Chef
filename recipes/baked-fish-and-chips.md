**Horseradish tartar sauce**

- Whisk the mayonnaise, capers, horseradish, dill, lemon juice and zest, a pinch of salt and some pepper. Cover and refrigerate.

**Toasted panko**

- Set racks in the top and bottom thirds of the oven. Put a large rimmed baking sheet on the lower rack and heat the oven to 500°F.
- In a large skillet over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F), warm 2 TBLS of the oil. Stir in the panko, thyme, garlic and 1/2 tsp pepper and cook, stirring often, until evenly dark golden, about 5 minutes. Move to a bowl right away.

**Egg wash**

- Whisk the mustard and eggs in a bowl. Put the flour in a third bowl.

**Bread the fish**

- Oil an oven-safe wire rack and set it over a second rimmed baking sheet. Season the fish with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.
- Dredge each piece in flour, then the mustard-egg, then the panko, coating well at each stage. Set on the rack. (Can be breaded up to 4 hours ahead, uncovered in the fridge.)

**Chips, then fish**

- Toss the potatoes with the remaining 4 TBLS oil, 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper. Spread carefully on the preheated sheet and roast on the lower rack, tossing after 15 minutes, until slightly golden and crisp.
- Lower the oven to 425°F. Put the fish, on its rack, on the top rack. Bake until the fish is flaky and golden (145°F internal) and the potatoes are browned and tender, 10-15 minutes more.
- Salt both right out of the oven. Serve hot with the tartar sauce.
