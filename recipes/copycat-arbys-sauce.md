**Make the sauce**

- Combine everything in `1 -> Sauce` in a small saucepan.
- Cook over medium-low heat (pan surface 250-300°F, a bare simmer) for 20 minutes, stirring often, especially in the last 10 minutes.
- The sauce thickens a little as it cools; add water a spoonful at a time to keep it pourable.
- Cool, then store in an airtight container in the fridge. Use within 1-2 weeks.

**Roast beef sandwiches**

- Lightly butter the inside of soft buns (King's Hawaiian sandwich buns work). Grill butter-side down in a large frying pan over medium heat until lightly golden.
- Warm shaved deli roast beef in the pan just until hot; don't overcook.
- Fill the buns with the beef, top with the sauce, and serve with curly fries.

**Notes**

- Doubles or triples easily.
- White vinegar works in place of cider vinegar with an extra pinch of brown sugar.
