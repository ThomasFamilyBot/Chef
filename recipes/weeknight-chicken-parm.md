**Sauce**

- Warm the olive oil in a pot over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F) and cook the onion until just translucent. Add the garlic and sauté 1-2 minutes, until fragrant.
- Add the tomatoes, 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper. Cook over medium-low about 15 minutes, until thickened and a little sweet. Adjust the seasoning.

**Breading**

- Beat the eggs in a large bowl until no streaks of white remain. On a plate, mix the panko, 3 TBLS Parmesan, 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.
- One at a time, dip the cutlets in egg, let the excess drip off, then press both sides firmly into the panko. Set on a clean plate.

**Cheese**

- Turn on the broiler, with the top rack as close to it as it goes. Put your largest cast-iron skillet under the broiler for 5 minutes.
- Meanwhile, mix the mozzarella with the remaining 4 TBLS Parmesan.

**Broil**

- With oven mitts, pull out the hot skillet. Add the avocado oil and the four cutlets in a single layer, spaced out. Broil 3-4 minutes, until the tops are crisp and golden; peek at 3 minutes and give it another minute if needed.
- Flip the cutlets. Top each with a few spoonfuls of sauce and a heavy sprinkle of the cheese. Broil 2 minutes more, until the cheese bubbles and browns in spots (chicken 165°F).
- Serve hot, with hunks of buttered bread.

**Notes**

- Nutrition counts the egg and panko at the house breading rates.
