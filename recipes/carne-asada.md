**Marinade**

- In a large non-reactive bowl or baking dish, whisk the olive oil, soy sauce, lime juice, vinegar, sugar, pepper and cumin. Stir in the garlic, jalapeño and cilantro.
- Optional: set aside a couple of TBLS before the meat goes in, to drizzle over the sliced steak.

**Marinate the steak**

- Turn the steak in the marinade a few times to coat. Cover and refrigerate 1-4 hours or overnight (flank steak: at least 3 hours).

**Grill**

- Set up the grill for two zones: high direct heat (grate 500°F+) on one side, low indirect on the other. Or use a cast iron grill pan over high heat (pan surface 450-500°F) with the vent on.
- Lift the steak out and lightly brush off most of the garlic and cilantro bits; leave the oil.
- Sear on the hot side a few minutes per side until well browned.
- Move to the cool side, thicker end toward the heat, and cook to temperature. Pull at 115-120°F for rare, 125°F medium-rare, 140°F medium. Flank is best rare; skirt can take more cooking.

**Rest & slice**

- Tent with foil and rest 10 minutes.
- Slice thin across the grain with a long sharp knife, angling the blade for wide slices.

**Serve**

- Warm the tortillas 30 seconds a side in a dry skillet or on the grill.
- Serve with avocado, lime wedges, radishes, lettuce and pico de gallo.

**Notes**

- Simply Recipes' own estimate: 495 calories per serving.
- Leftovers keep 3-4 days; reheat in a little oil or in the air fryer at 350°F to 140°F.
