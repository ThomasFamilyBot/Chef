**The night before: assemble**

- Butter a 2-quart baking dish. Butter the front side of each bread slice and shingle the slices upright in the dish, overlapping as needed. Tuck the raisins, if using, between the slices, not on top.

**Custard**

- Whisk the eggs, milk, cream, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, vanilla and salt until smooth. Pour over the bread and press gently so it soaks in.
- Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate overnight.

**Bake**

- Heat the oven to 350°F. Uncover and scatter the pecans over the top.
- Bake until puffed, golden and set in the center, 50-60 minutes; tent with foil if the top darkens too fast. Stand 10 minutes.

**Serve**

- Dust with confectioners' sugar and serve with maple syrup.

**Notes**

- A stale loaf is ideal: brioche, fruit-filled panettone or braided challah. Not croissants; their chewy insides are best fresh.
