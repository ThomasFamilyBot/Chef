**Make the caramel**

- In a medium pot over medium heat (pan surface about 300-350°F), melt 3/4 cup of the sugar with the water, swirling the pot often, until it turns mahogany brown, almost but not quite black (about 350-360°F on a thermometer). Pull it off the heat just before you think it's ready; it keeps darkening.
- Keep extra sugar on hand in case the first batch burns.

**Make the custard**

- Add the cream, milk, remaining 1/2 cup sugar and the fine salt. Simmer (185-205°F) until the caramel melts and the mixture is completely smooth. Remove from the heat.
- In a separate bowl, whisk the yolks. Whisking constantly, slowly whisk in about a third of the hot cream, then whisk the yolk mixture back into the pot.
- Return to medium-low heat and cook gently until thick enough to coat the back of a spoon, about 170°F.
- Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a bowl. Cool to room temperature, cover, and chill at least 4 hours or overnight.

**Churn**

- Churn in an ice cream machine per the manufacturer's instructions. Sprinkle in the flaky salt during the last 2 minutes.
- Serve straight from the machine for soft serve, or freeze until needed.

**Notes**

- Lighter version: more milk, less cream, as long as the dairy totals 3 cups. Fewer yolks make a thinner base; don't go below three.
- NYT's own estimate: 306 calories per serving.
