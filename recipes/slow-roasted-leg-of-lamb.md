**Prepare the lamb**

- Heat the oven to 450°F.
- Cut 20 slits in the lamb and push a garlic half into each. Rub with the olive oil and season all over with the salt and pepper.

**Roast uncovered**

- Set the lamb fat side up in a large heavy pot with a lid (a big Dutch oven) and roast uncovered at 450°F for 30 minutes.

**Braise covered**

- Add the thyme, rosemary, oregano, lemon juice, wine and stock. Cover, lower the oven to 350°F and cook 3 hours.
- Check near the end; if the liquid has cooked away, add more stock.

**Brown the top**

- Uncover and cook 30 minutes more at 350°F, until the top is browned. Add stock if the pot is dry.

**Finish & serve**

- Skim the fat from the pan juices for a sauce and season with more salt, pepper and lemon juice.
- Serve with lemon wedges.

**Notes**

- The source doesn't give a doneness temperature: this is a slow braise-roast to tender, well past medium-rare (expect 190°F+ internal).
