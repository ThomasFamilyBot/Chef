**Spice mix**

- In a food processor, pulse the peanuts, paprika, ginger, garlic powder, onion powder, sugar, cayenne, 3/4 tsp salt and the pepper until finely ground, about 20 seconds.
- Set aside 1/3 cup of the spice mix. Move the rest to a medium bowl, add the oil and stir to a paste.

**Season the beef**

- In a large bowl, toss the beef with 1 tsp salt, massaging until evenly coated. Add the paste and toss and massage it into the meat.

**Skewer and broil**

- Thread the beef tightly onto four 12-inch metal skewers, several pieces per skewer, packed close together.
- Set the skewers on a wire rack in a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet. Heat the broiler with a rack about 4 inches from the element.
- Broil until well browned, about 5 minutes, flipping halfway. The center of a piece should be medium-rare (130-135°F). Rest 5 minutes.

**Finish**

- Brush the lime juice over both sides of the skewers, then sprinkle with the reserved spice mix. Serve with lime wedges.

**Notes**

- The Nigerian street snack, eaten wrapped in newspaper; the peanut and red-pepper spice rub is what sets it apart.
- Flat iron steaks may be labeled top blade; if a line of gristle runs down the center, cut it out before slicing (it splits the steak in two).
- Good with cucumber, cabbage and onion to cool the heat. Don't skip the lime wedges.
- Chicken version: use an equal weight of boneless skinless chicken thighs, sliced lengthwise into 1/2-inch strips; they come out crisp outside and tender inside.
