**Dredge**

- Heat the oven to 375°F.
- Pat the shanks dry with paper towels. Season both sides with a pinch of salt and pepper and coat in flour, shaking off the excess.

**Brown**

- Set a Dutch oven over medium-high heat (pan surface 375-425°F). Once hot, drizzle in the oil and add the shanks.
- Brown 2-3 minutes per side, then lift them out and set aside.

**Vegetables**

- In the same pot, add the onion, carrot, celery, garlic and oregano and season with about 1 tsp each salt and pepper.
- Cook about 5 minutes, until the onion is soft. Add 1 cup stock (or white wine) and deglaze, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom.

**Braise**

- Stir in the remaining 1 cup stock and the crushed tomatoes.
- Return the shanks to the pot, cover with the lid, and move to the oven. Cook 2 hours, or until the beef is fork-tender (about 200°F internal).

**Serve**

- Serve the shanks with the sauce from the pot, garnished with the parsley.

**Notes**

- 375°F is hot for a braise; if the liquid is bubbling hard when you check at an hour, drop the oven to 325°F and give it an extra 30 minutes.
- Nutrition: flour at the house dredge rate (30%); shank at about 70% edible after the bone (USDA has only a lean-only raw record for shank, so the marrow isn't counted).
