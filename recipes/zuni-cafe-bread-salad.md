**Plump the currants**

- Soak the currants in the red wine vinegar and warm water for about 10 minutes (`1 -> Plump the currants`).
- Have the chicken roasting so it comes out of the oven while the bread is still warm: the family's [Zuni Spice Rub Roast Chicken](https://thomasfamilybot.github.io/Chef/r/zuni-spice-rub-roast-chicken.html) or [Barbara Kafka's Simplest Roast Chicken](https://food52.com/recipes/17568-barbara-kafka-s-simplest-roast-chicken).

**Toast the bread**

- Heat the broiler. Carve off the bottom and most of the top and side crusts (save them for croutons). Tear the bread into irregular 2-3 inch chunks; you should have about 4 cups.
- Toss with 1-2 TBLS of the olive oil to coat lightly and broil very briefly, 3-4 inches from the element, just to color the edges.
- Toast the pine nuts on the broiler tray too (recommended), watching closely; they burn fast.

**Dress the bread**

- Whisk about 1/4 cup of the olive oil with the Champagne vinegar, salt and pepper (`3 -> Vinaigrette`).
- In a wide salad bowl, toss the warm bread with about 1/4 cup of the vinaigrette; it will be unevenly dressed.
- Taste one of the more saturated pieces. If bland, add salt and pepper and toss again.

**Add the garlic & scallions**

- Heat a spoonful of the olive oil in a small skillet over medium-low heat (pan surface about 250-300°F). Add the garlic and scallions and cook, stirring constantly, until softened without coloring.
- Scrape into the bread and fold to combine.
- Drain the currants and fold them in with the pine nuts.
- Dribble the chicken stock over the salad and fold again.

**Finish**

- Taste a saturated piece and a dry one. If bland, add salt, pepper and/or a few drops of vinegar and toss.
- When the chicken comes out of the oven, drizzle the bread with a spoonful or two of its pan juices and toss.
- Add the greens and a drizzle of vinaigrette, fold well, and taste again.
- Pile the salad on a platter, carve the chicken, and set the pieces on top, moistening the bread with more pan juices as needed.
