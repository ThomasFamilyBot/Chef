**Shake**

- Add the lemon juice, cinnamon syrup, banana liqueur, both rums and a dash of bitters to a shaker with ice.
- Shake hard and pour over ice.

**Notes**

- Cinnamon syrup: simmer 1 cup sugar and 1 cup water with 2-3 cinnamon sticks until the sugar dissolves, then steep off the heat 30 minutes and strain. Keeps 2 weeks in the fridge.
- The card just says "bitters"; Angostura is assumed.
- Nutrition: the overproof rum is valued as a Wray & Nephew-style rum (63% alcohol); a 75%-alcohol rum adds about 20 calories.
