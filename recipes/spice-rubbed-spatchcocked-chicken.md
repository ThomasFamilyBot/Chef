**Spatchcock**

- With poultry shears or a sharp knife, cut along one side of the backbone until the chicken opens. Cut along the other side and remove the backbone, or leave it attached.
- Open the bird flat, breast up, and press hard on the center of the breast until you feel a pop and it lies more or less flat.

**Rub**

- Mix the sugar, salt, chile powder, paprika, mustard powder, oregano, pepper, allspice and bay leaves. Smear all over the chicken.
- Lay skin side up on a rimmed baking sheet and refrigerate uncovered at least 2 hours and up to 24.

**Roast**

- Heat the oven to 425°F.
- Roast until the juices run clear when the thickest part of the thigh is pricked and the thickest part of the breast reads 150°F, 40-50 minutes.
- Tent with foil and rest 10 minutes before carving. Serve with lime or lemon wedges.
