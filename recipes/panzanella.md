**Toast the bread**

- Heat the oven to 425°F.
- Spread the bread cubes on a rimmed baking sheet and toss with 2 TBLS of the oil and a pinch of salt.
- Bake until dried out and pale golden at the edges, 7-15 minutes. Cool on a wire rack. Dry them thoroughly or the salad turns to mush.

**Dress the tomatoes**

- Cut the tomatoes into bite-size pieces and put them in a large bowl.
- Add the mozzarella, onion, garlic paste, 1 TBLS of the vinegar, the oregano, 1/4 tsp salt and the red pepper flakes, if using. Toss to coat and set aside.

**Make the dressing**

- In a medium bowl, combine the remaining 1 TBLS vinegar, the mustard, 1/4 tsp salt and black pepper to taste.
- Whisking constantly, slowly drizzle in the remaining 4 TBLS oil until thickened.
- Stir in the cucumber, basil and parsley.

**Assemble**

- Add the bread cubes, the cucumber mixture and the capers to the tomatoes and toss well.
- Let sit at least 30 minutes and up to 4 hours before serving (the recipe headnote says up to about 6).
- Just before serving, toss with a little more oil, vinegar and salt if needed.

**Notes**

- Built for overripe, oozing tomatoes at the height of the season.
- NYT's own estimate: 289 calories per serving.
