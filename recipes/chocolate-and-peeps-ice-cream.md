- **Make base:** Combine all of Mise Grouping `2` AND the 5 whole Peeps in a saucepan, heat on medium-high. Get to 165°F, keep stirring as it might bubble up on you. Peeps should be fully melted.
- **Temper yolks:** Heat still medium-low. In a separate bowl, mix yolks. Add a couple spoonfuls of ice cream base to the yolks, whisk. Then gradually whisk the yolk mixture into the ice cream base (you're doing it slowly so the eggs don't scramble). Target 172°F.
- **Melt chocolate:** Turn heat to low. Gradually add the chocolate from Mise Grouping `1`, stir until chocolate is fully melted.
- **Strain and cool:** Strain mixture into a bowl (fine mesh strainer). Place bowl into an ice bath to cool. OK to put ice cream base in fridge for a couple hours; do NOT let it sit overnight (texture on the chocolate gets grainy).
- **Churn:** Make ice cream in your mixer. KitchenAid mixer: level 1. After ~15 min, when the ice cream appears to be set, add the 5 chopped Peeps. Right before turning off the mixer, add sugar crystals to taste (gives it a little crunch).
