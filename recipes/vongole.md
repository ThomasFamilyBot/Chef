- Heavy saucepan, med heat. Saute garlic & parsley in oil. Do NOT allow garlic to brown.
- Add tomatoes & clams.
- Season w/ salt, pepper and chili flakes.
- Turn heat to low, cover for 30-40 min.
- Cook the spaghetti and toss with the sauce.
