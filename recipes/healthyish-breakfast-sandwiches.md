**Roasted onion**

- Heat the oven to 350°F. On a large rimmed baking sheet, toss the onion rings with the soy sauce, garlic powder and 1 TBLS oil; season with salt.
- Bake, tossing once halfway, until the onion is browned at the edges and very tender, 25-35 minutes. Cool 5 minutes.

**Herb sauce**

- Meanwhile, stir the herbs, hot sauce and remaining 1 TBLS oil in a small bowl. Salt if it needs it.

**Eggs**

- Melt the butter in a small nonstick skillet over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F). Add the eggs, season with salt, and cook, stirring with a rubber spatula, until they set into creamy folds, 1-2 minutes.

**Assemble**

- Spread the herb sauce on the cut sides of the muffins. Set the bottom halves on a rimmed baking sheet, top with the eggs, then lay the cheese over.
- Bake just until the cheese melts, about 5 minutes. Pile on the onion and close with the muffin tops.

**Notes**

- The slow-roasted onion stands in for bacon: jammy, crisp-edged and full of umami.
- Do ahead: the onion keeps 5 days in the fridge. For a no-oven sandwich, fold the cheese into the eggs just as they start to set, top with cold onion, and assemble on toasted muffins.
