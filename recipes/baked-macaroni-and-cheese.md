**Cook the pasta**

- Heat the oven to 350°F.
- Cook the macaroni in a large pot of boiling salted water (212°F) to al dente. Drain.

**Make the sauce**

- While the pasta cooks, melt the butter in a separate pot over medium heat (pan surface about 300-350°F).
- Whisk in the flour and mustard and keep it moving for about 5 minutes, until free of lumps.
- Stir in the milk, onion, bay leaf and paprika. Simmer (185-205°F) for 10 minutes, then remove the bay leaf.

**Finish the sauce**

- Temper in the egg: whisk a ladle of the hot sauce into the beaten egg, then stir the egg back into the pot. Keep it below 175°F so it doesn't scramble.
- Stir in 3/4 of the cheese. Season with the salt and pepper.
- Fold in the macaroni and pour into a 2-quart casserole dish. Scatter the remaining cheese on top.

**Topping and bake**

- Melt the butter in a sauté pan and toss the panko to coat. Spread over the macaroni.
- Bake 30 minutes, until bubbling at the edges and golden on top.
- Rest 5 minutes before serving.

**Notes**

- Alton says to save the leftovers for fried macaroni and cheese.
