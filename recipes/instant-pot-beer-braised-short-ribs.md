**Pressure-cook the short ribs**

- Season the short ribs generously with salt and pepper on all sides.
- Nestle them in the Instant Pot with the halved jalapeño, garlic and beer.
- Lock the lid and close the pressure valve. Pressure Cook on High for 40 minutes.
- Flick the valve to release the pressure (mind the steam) and let it vent about 15 minutes. The meat should be fork-tender (about 200°F internal).

**Mint sauce**

- Meanwhile, stir the mint, hot water, vinegar, sugar and salt together.
- Taste: more vinegar, sugar or salt until it's bright and right. Serve spooned over the ribs.

**Notes**

- Good with white rice and a cold beer. The mint sauce is the point; the aromatics are optional.
