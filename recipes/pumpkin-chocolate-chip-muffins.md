**Mix the wet ingredients**

- Heat the oven to 400°F. Grease a 12-cup muffin pan or line it with paper liners.
- In a medium bowl, whisk the sugar, eggs and oil together. Stir in the pumpkin and water until well combined.

**Mix the dry ingredients**

- In a large bowl, mix the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves and salt.

**Combine and bake**

- Stir the pumpkin mixture into the flour mixture until just combined; don't overmix. Fold in the chocolate chips.
- Divide the batter among the muffin cups, filling each about 2/3 full.
- Bake until a toothpick in the center comes out clean, 20-25 minutes.
- Cool in the pan a few minutes, then move to a wire rack to cool completely.

**Notes**

- Up to 1 cup of chips works; milk or white chocolate chips make them sweeter.
- Makes 36 mini muffins instead: bake 10-14 minutes.
- Allrecipes' own estimate: 197 calories per muffin.
