**Dressing**

- Whisk the buttermilk, sour cream, mayonnaise, garlic, shallot, mustard, tarragon, dill, parsley, chives, pepper, hot sauce and lemon juice in a mixing bowl.
- Move to an airtight container and refrigerate at least 1 day before using so the flavors marry. Keep leftovers in the fridge.

**Notes**

- Make it a day or two ahead: the dried herbs need an overnight soak to steep, like tea, and come to life.
