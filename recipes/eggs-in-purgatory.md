**Tomato sauce**

- Heat 1/4 cup oil in a medium skillet over medium-high (pan surface 375-425°F). When it just shimmers, add the garlic and season generously with kosher salt and pepper. Cook, stirring constantly, until the garlic is just turning golden at the edges, about 2 minutes.
- Stir in the red pepper flakes, then the tomatoes. Cook, tossing now and then, until the tomatoes plump and some skins split, about 2 minutes.
- Reduce to medium (300-350°F), cover, and cook, stirring every couple of minutes and lightly smashing the tomatoes with a wooden spoon, until saucy and slightly deflated but still holding some shape, 5-7 minutes.

**Eggs**

- Reduce the heat to medium-low so the sauce bubbles gently (a bare simmer, 180-190°F). Crack the eggs into a large measuring cup, then slip them one at a time into the sauce, spaced around the edge of the pan.
- Season the eggs with salt and pepper, cover, and cook until the whites are set but the yolks still runny, 4-6 minutes.

**Toasts**

- While the eggs cook, toast the bread until crisp. Drizzle with oil, then rub the outside of the whole lemon firmly over the toasts to release the oils in the peel. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

**Salad**

- Squeeze about 2 TBLS lemon juice over the chard leaves in a medium bowl, add the basil, drizzle with oil, and season with kosher salt and pepper. Toss.

**Serve**

- Scoop each egg out from underneath to keep the yolk whole and set in a shallow bowl. Spoon sauce around it and sprinkle with flaky salt. Serve with the salad and toasts.

**Notes**

- Any tomatoes work; cherry tomatoes hold their shape best. Ripe beefsteaks or Romas: cut into 1-inch pieces and carry on.
- Do ahead: the tomato sauce keeps a day in the fridge. Reheat to a simmer before adding the eggs.
