**Warm the nuts**

- Heat the oven to 375°F.
- Spread the cashews on an ungreased baking sheet and bake about 10 minutes, until warmed through. They should not take on much color.

**Season**

- Meanwhile, combine the rosemary, cayenne, brown sugar, salt and melted butter in a large bowl (`2 -> Seasoning`).
- Toss the warm nuts in the seasoning until completely coated.
- Serve warm.

**Notes**

- Almost directly from the bar nuts at Union Square Cafe in New York.
