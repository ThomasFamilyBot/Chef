**Yolk mixture**

- In a large bowl, whisk the yolks, sugar, honey, vanilla and salt until smooth.

**Make the custard**

- In a medium saucepan over medium heat (pan surface about 300-350°F), bring the cream to a simmer (185-205°F).
- Whisking the yolks constantly, slowly pour in half the hot cream. Scrape the custard back into the saucepan.
- Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until thick enough to coat the back of a spoon, 180°F on a thermometer. Don't let it simmer or it can curdle.
- Strain into a heatproof bowl and whisk in the cream cheese until completely melted.
- Set the bowl in a larger bowl of ice water. With a hand mixer or immersion blender, whip the custard until thick and cold, about 5 minutes.
- Spoon into 3 or 4 ice cube trays. Freeze at least 3 hours or overnight (up to 1 week).

**Soft serve**

- Pop out the cubes into a food processor or blender. Pulse with the milk until smooth and the consistency of soft serve.
- Serve immediately, or return to a container and freeze up to 1 week; take it out 20 minutes before serving.

**Notes**

- No ice cream maker needed. The cream cheese (a Jeni's trick) holds the emulsion and keeps ice crystals down.
- With an ice cream maker: cool the custard completely and churn it instead of freezing in trays.
