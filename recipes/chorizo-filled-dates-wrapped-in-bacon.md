**Stuff the dates**

- Slice the chorizo crosswise into thirds. Halve each piece lengthwise, then cut each half into 4 lengthwise strips: 24 small sticks.
- Tuck a chorizo stick into each date from `1 -> Stuff the dates` and pinch closed.

**Wrap & cook**

- Wrap a half-slice of bacon around each date and secure with a toothpick.
- Set them seam side down in a large skillet and sauté over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F), turning, until the bacon is browned on all sides, about 10 minutes.
- Drain on paper towels and serve hot.

**Notes**

- Use dry-cured Spanish chorizo; raw Mexican chorizo won't hold as a stick.
- Reviewer alternative: bake seam side down on a rack over a sheet pan at 375°F, 10-15 minutes, until the bacon is as crisp as you like. They reheat well.
