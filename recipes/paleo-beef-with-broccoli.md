**Marinate the beef**

- Slice the beef about 1/4 inch thin. Mix well with everything in `1 -> Marinate the beef`: 2 TBLS coconut aminos, 1/2 tsp salt, 1 TBLS sesame oil, 1/4 tsp pepper, the arrowroot and baking soda.

**Broccoli**

- Put the florets in a microwave-safe container with 1-2 TBLS water. Cover loosely with a lid or wet paper towel and microwave 2 minutes, until tender but still crunchy. Set aside.

**Aromatics**

- Heat a wok over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F) with the ghee. When hot, add the garlic and ginger, season with a small pinch of salt, and stir-fry until fragrant, about 10 seconds.

**Cook the beef**

- Turn the heat up to medium-high (375-425°F) and add the marinated beef, spreading it evenly over the bottom of the pan. Cook until the edges darken and crisp.
- Flip and do the same on the other side, until it's about 3/4 cooked through with a slightly charred, crispy surface.

**Sauce combo**

- Add the remaining 2 TBLS coconut aminos, the fish sauce, 2 tsp sesame oil and 1/4 tsp pepper. Stir-fry about 1 minute.
- Add the broccoli and stir-fry 30 seconds more, tossing everything to combine.

**Notes**

- The baking soda in the marinade tenderizes the beef (velveting), so a lean cut stays silky.
- Good for make-ahead meals.
