**Chicken**

- Put the oil in a large, heavy skillet over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F). Season the thighs with salt and pepper and lay them in skin side down.
- Cook without moving them until the fat has rendered and the skin is a rich golden brown and crisp, 15-30 minutes. Fiddle with the heat, lowering it to medium-low (250-300°F) if the skin starts to burn before it's evenly golden.

**Finish**

- Turn the thighs over and stir the preserved lemon rind into the fat in the skillet.
- Keep cooking until the meat closest to the bone is cooked through (175°F), about 15 minutes more.
- Serve the thighs and the lemony pan drippings with lemon wedges.

**Notes**

- This is Paul Bertolli's "bottom-up cooking", streamlined by Melissa Hamilton and Christopher Hirsheimer of Canal House: skin side down in a barely hot pan, left mostly alone for about 30 minutes, turned once. Thighs lie flat and jigsaw neatly into a round skillet.
- Canal House variations: sherry and mushrooms, or bacon and olives. Or deglaze and make a pan sauce.
- Nutrition counts the thighs with skin at about 78% edible after the bone; the rendered fat left in the pan isn't subtracted.
