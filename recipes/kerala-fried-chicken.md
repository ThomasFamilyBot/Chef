**Day 1: marinade**

- In a blender, purée the buttermilk, garlic, serranos, cilantro, mint and 2 TBLS salt until smooth.
- Put the chicken in a lidded container, pour the marinade over, and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate at least 18 hours and up to 24.

**Day 2: fry**

- Fill a large cast-iron skillet with 1 inch of oil and heat gently over medium until it reaches 350°F. Set a cooling rack over a rimmed baking sheet.

**Dredge**

- Mix the flour and 1 tsp salt in a shallow dish. Lift the chicken from the marinade, shake off the excess, and dredge each piece thoroughly.

**Fry and finish**

- Fry without crowding until deep golden and cooked through, about 4 minutes per side, 165°F internal. Drain on the rack and drizzle with the melted coconut oil.
- Dip the curry leaves in the hot oil until crisp, 10-15 seconds, and set on the rack to garnish.

**Notes**

- Nutrition counts the marinade, frying oil and dredge at the house partial-use rates.
