**Sauce**

- Mix the garlic, honey, ketchup, soy sauce, vinegar and oregano. Pour over the chicken and stir to coat.

**Cook**

- Slow cooker: 3 hours on high. Pressure cooker or Instant Pot: 20 minutes at high pressure, then release the pressure manually.
- No slow cooker or Instant Pot? Nestle the coated chicken in an ovenproof skillet or Dutch oven and bake at 425°F for 20 minutes (175°F internal).

**Garnish**

- Top with the sesame seeds and scallions. Good over greens with rice, or mashed sweet potatoes.
