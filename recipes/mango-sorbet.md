**Purée**

- Pack the diced mango into a blender with the water and blend at full speed until very smooth, about 30 seconds.
- Push through a fine-mesh strainer with a spoon into a large measuring cup until you have 3 cups of purée. Save the rest for something else.

**Sweeten**

- Transfer the purée to a large bowl and whisk in the sugar until dissolved.
- Whisk in the lime juice and salt a little at a time, tasting as you go.

**Chill and churn**

- Refrigerate until very cold, 2-3 hours.
- Churn per your machine's instructions, then pack into an airtight container and freeze at least 4 hours.

**Notes**

- From Serious Eats' "The Science of the Best Sorbet": the salt and lime are there to sharpen the mango, so adjust by taste.
- Serious Eats' own estimate: 161 calories per serving.
