**Stir**

- Fill a mixing glass with ice. Add the gin, Cocchi Americano and dry vermouth.
- Stir until chilled and slightly diluted, about 15 seconds.
- Set one large ice cube in a rocks glass and strain the drink over it.

**Garnish**

- Garnish with the two cranberries and the rosemary sprig (poke a hole through each cranberry with a skewer and thread them onto the sprig). For the holidays, rim a quarter of the glass with sparkly sugar or candy-cane dust.

**Notes**

- A clear-colored Negroni: Cocchi Americano stands in for Campari with light citrus, floral notes and gentle bitterness; dry vermouth adds the herbal depth.
- Frozen version: blend the spirits with crushed ice to a slush, adding another 1/2 fl oz gin to stand up to the dilution.
