**Glaze broth**

- Set up the grill two-zone with the cool (indirect) side at 225°F.
- Heat the chicken stock with 4 TBLS of the glaze in a small pan until the glaze dissolves and it steams.

**Smoke**

- Stand the ham cut side down on the indirect side and smoke 30 minutes.

**Wrap**

- Tear off 6 feet of foil and fold it in half. Set the flat (cut) side of the ham in the center and crimp the sides up so it will hold liquid.
- Pour in half the glaze broth, then seal the ham like a Hershey's Kiss; you don't want any liquid or steam to escape.
- Back on the indirect side, insert a probe thermometer through the foil into the thickest part. Turn the grill up if you're short on time.

**Glaze and finish**

- When the ham reaches 130°F (about 3 hours), open the foil and paint on more glaze. Close the lid for 10 minutes.
- Pour the juices from the foil back into the pan with the rest of the glaze broth and keep warm for a dipping sauce.
- Finish the ham over direct heat, about 30 seconds per side, to caramelize the glaze. Carve and serve with the warm sauce.

**Notes**

- A city ham is already cooked, so 130°F is a reheat target, not a safety one; going much higher dries it out.
- Nutrition assumes an 8 lb bone-in half ham (about 80% edible after the bone) and about 1/2 cup of a sugar-based glaze in all; the broth is counted as served.
