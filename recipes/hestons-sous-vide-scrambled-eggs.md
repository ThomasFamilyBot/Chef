**Egg mixture**

- Set the sous vide bath to 167°F (75°C).
- Whisk the eggs, milk and cream in a medium bowl. Whisk in the warm melted butter and season with salt and pepper.

**Cook**

- Divide the mixture between two small zip-top bags. Seal with the water-displacement method, lower into the bath, and clip the bags to the side of the pot. Cook 15 minutes.
- Every 3-5 minutes, lift the bags out and gently massage the eggs to mix, then return them to the bath.

**Brown butter**

- Meanwhile, melt the remaining 2 TBLS butter in a small saucepan over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F). Keep cooking, stirring constantly, until it stops foaming and the milk solids turn golden brown, about 5 minutes.
- Strain through a coffee filter or towel-lined sieve into a small bowl. Keep warm.

**Serve**

- When the timer goes, take the eggs out of the bags onto two plates. Drizzle with the brown butter and serve with toast.

**Notes**

- Inspired by Heston Blumenthal's sous vide scrambled eggs: smooth, light and creamy. Indulgent (milk, cream, butter and brown butter), so a Sunday thing.
- Don't substitute lower-fat dairy; the eggs come out runny.
- Good alongside buttery mushrooms, pan-cooked tomatoes, smoked salmon or chives.
