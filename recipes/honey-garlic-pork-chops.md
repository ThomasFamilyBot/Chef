**Sear**

- Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high (pan surface 375-425°F). Season the chops with salt and pepper.
- When the skillet is hot, sear the chops uncovered 3 minutes per side. Set aside.

**Sauce**

- Turn the heat to low-medium (pan surface 250-300°F). Add the butter and garlic and cook about a minute.
- Stir in the honey, water, vinegar and red pepper flakes. Cook a few minutes, until the sauce starts to thicken and bubble.
- Return the chops and cook 5-10 minutes, basting often, until 145°F internal. Rest 3 minutes.
- Slice and serve over rice with the remaining sauce; garnish with parsley and more red pepper flakes if you like.

**Notes**

- Garlic swaps: 2 TBLS minced = 3/4 tsp garlic powder or 1 1/2 tsp granulated garlic, added with the honey and water.
- Thin or boneless chops cook faster. If the sauce stays thin, a little cornstarch slurry fixes it.
