**Pre-ferment**

- Whisk the yeast with 1 cup (130 g) of the flour in a small bowl. In a small saucepan over medium-low (pan surface 250-300°F), warm the buttermilk with 1/4 cup water, stirring often, to lukewarm (about 100°F), about 5 minutes.
- Pour the buttermilk into the flour and whisk smooth. Cover and let sit 1 hour; this gives deeper flavor and extra rise.

**Maple-honey butter**

- Meanwhile, whisk 1/2 cup softened butter with 3 TBLS honey, 2 TBLS maple syrup and 3/4 tsp salt until smooth. Keep at room temperature if using within a few hours; otherwise refrigerate up to a week and soften and re-whisk before serving.

**Maple-honey syrup**

- In a small saucepan, combine 1/2 cup butter, 1/3 cup honey, 1 1/2 TBLS maple syrup, the soy sauce and 1/2 tsp salt. Heat over medium-low, whisking often, until the butter melts completely.
- Whisking, add 1 1/2 TBLS water and keep whisking until emulsified. Hold on the lowest heat.

**Berry compote**

- Gently mix the berries, 1/3 cup sugar and the cornstarch in a large bowl until coated. Heat a large deep skillet over high heat (pan surface 450-500°F) until a drop of water sizzles away instantly.
- Add the berries and cook, stirring once in a while, until the blueberries look about to pop, about 2 minutes. Take off the heat.

**Batter**

- Heat the oven to 350°F with a rack in the center and a metal rack set in a sheet pan on it.
- Once the pre-ferment has proofed an hour, whisk the remaining 1 cup flour with 2 TBLS sugar, the baking soda and 1/2 tsp salt. In a large bowl whisk the eggs and oil smooth. Scrape in the pre-ferment, add the dry ingredients, and stir gently with the whisk just until no dry streaks remain. Lumps are fine.

**Cook the pancakes**

- Heat one or two 7- to 8-inch nonstick skillets (5-6 inches across the bottom) over medium (300-350°F) until very hot; no grease needed. Fill each pan with batter about 1/3 inch deep and smooth the top so it reaches the edges in a neat round.
- Cook until the bottom is crisp and evenly golden, 2-4 minutes, lowering the heat if it colors too fast. Flip and cook until the second side is crisp and golden, 1-3 minutes, tucking in the edges for a nice dome.
- Move the pancake to the rack in the oven so the center cooks through, 2-5 minutes; a paring knife poked into the middle should find no wet batter. Repeat, reheating the pan between pancakes; earlier batches can wait in the oven.

**Serve**

- Center one or two hot pancakes on each plate and drench right away with the maple-honey syrup. Spoon the berry compote on top, then a scoop of maple-honey butter over the berries. Zest the lemon over everything and serve immediately.

**Notes**

- Sam Yoo's Chinatown diner pancakes: a yeast-risen buttermilk batter cooked in individual skillets for soufflé-pancake height, drenched in buttery maple-honey syrup, topped with salted honey-maple butter (a nod to Korean honey-butter chips) and a tangy berry compote.
