**Marinate the beef**

- Toss the beef cubes with 1/2 tsp of the black pepper and 1 TBLS of the oil. Marinate about 2 hours.

**Stir-fry sauce**

- Meanwhile, combine the vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, fish sauce and rice wine. Stir until the sugar dissolves. Set aside.

**Stir-fry (two batches)**

- Divide the beef and the onions into two portions. Don't crowd the pan or the meat won't brown; split into more batches if your pan or burner is small.
- Heat 1/4 cup oil in a wok or large sauté pan over maximum heat (pan surface 500°F+, oil just at its smoke point).
- When the oil smokes, add the first batch of beef in one layer. Let a brown crust form, then turn to brown the other side, 3-4 minutes total (medium-rare, 125-130°F internal).
- Add half the red onion, scallions and garlic. Shake or stir about 30 seconds.
- Add half the stir-fry sauce and keep shaking the pan.
- Add 1 TBLS butter and shake until it melts. Remove from the heat.
- Wash the pan and repeat with the second batch.

**Lime dipping sauce**

- Mix the salt and the remaining 1/2 tsp pepper in a ramekin. Pour the lime juice over.

**Serve**

- Pile the beef over the watercress and serve with the dipping sauce.

**Notes**

- Charles Phan's bò lúc lắc, the Slanted Door signature. The 7x7 page gives the sauce as one batch; split it between the two rounds of beef.
