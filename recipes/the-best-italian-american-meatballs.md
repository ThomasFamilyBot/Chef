**Gelled stock (optional)**

- Sprinkle the gelatin over the stock in a heatproof measuring cup and let stand 5 minutes. Microwave 1-2 minutes, stirring once or twice, until dissolved.
- Pour into a wide heatproof bowl and refrigerate until fully set, about 30 minutes. (Skipping this still gives very moist meatballs.)

**Panade**

- In the stand mixer bowl, toss the bread with the buttermilk and let stand about 10 minutes, tossing now and then.
- Squeeze or mash to check for dry spots; add more buttermilk 1 TBLS at a time until moist throughout.

**Flavorings**

- Add everything from `3 -> Flavorings` to the bread (saving the extra Parmesan for serving). Finely mince the gelled stock, if using, and add it.
- With the paddle, beat from low up to medium-high until thoroughly blended, about 1 minute, scraping down the sides.

**Meat**

- Add 1/3 of the beef and 1/3 of the pork and beat at medium-high until completely blended into the bread mixture.
- Take the bowl off the mixer. Add the rest of the meat and mix gently by hand, teasing the meat apart with your fingers, just until there are no unmixed pockets. Don't overmix.

**Broil**

- Set a rack in the upper position, heat the broiler, and line 2 rimmed baking sheets with foil.
- Form tight handball-sized balls, about 2 inches wide and 3-3 1/4 oz each (about 20).
- Broil one sheet at a time until browned on top, 7-10 minutes (broilers vary a lot).

**Sauce**

- Bring the tomato sauce to a simmer (185-205°F) in a medium pot.

**Simmer & serve**

- Add half the meatballs and simmer until just cooked through, about 145°F internal, 10-12 minutes. Move to a serving bowl and repeat with the rest. Longer simmering costs juice.
- Spoon sauce over and grate more Parmesan on top.

**Notes**

- Serious Eats pulls these at 145°F; USDA FSIS guidance for ground beef and pork is 160°F.
- Sauce nutrition is counted in its own recipe: [Quick and Easy Italian-American Red Sauce](https://thomasfamilybot.github.io/Chef/r/quick-and-easy-italian-american-red-sauce.html).
- Veal version: 2/3 lb each of beef, pork and veal.
