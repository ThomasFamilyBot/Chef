**Toast the garlic**

- Combine `1 -> Toast the garlic` in a medium pot and cook over low heat (pan surface 200-250°F), stirring occasionally, until the garlic is lightly golden, 2-3 minutes.

**Bloom the spices**

- Add `2 -> Bloom the spices` and cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute.

**Simmer the sauce**

- Add everything from `3 -> Simmer the sauce` and bring to a boil (212°F).
- Reduce the heat and simmer gently (185-195°F), stirring frequently so it doesn't burn, until reduced to 4 cups, 20-30 minutes.
- Remove from the heat and let cool.
- Keeps up to 1 week refrigerated in an airtight container.
