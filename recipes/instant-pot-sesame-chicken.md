**Chicken**

- Toss the chicken strips with the pepper and 1 TBLS arrowroot.

**Sear**

- On a 6-quart Instant Pot, Sauté at Normal/Medium with the avocado oil. Cook half the chicken, stirring once, until golden, about 6 minutes; move to a plate and repeat. Press Cancel.

**Sauce**

- Whisk the broth, coconut aminos, vinegar, sesame oil, fish sauce and the remaining 1 tsp arrowroot until dissolved. Add the red pepper flakes if using.

**Pressure cook**

- Return all the chicken to the pot and pour the sauce over. Lock the lid, Manual for 6 minutes, quick release.
- Stir and let stand 5-10 minutes to thicken. Meanwhile, cook the broccoli per the package (fresh florets: microwave covered with 3 TBLS water, 3 minutes, until crisp-tender).

**Serve**

- Chicken over the broccoli, with sesame seeds and scallions on top.

**Notes**

- Toast sesame seeds in a dry skillet over medium, stirring, until fragrant and lightly browned, about 2 minutes.
