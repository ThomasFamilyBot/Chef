**Anchovy mayo**

- Set a rack in the lower third of the oven and heat to 450°F.
- Whisk the anchovies, garlic, 3 TBLS mayonnaise, 1 TBLS mustard, 1 TBLS oil and 1 1/2 tsp pepper. Set aside 1 TBLS in a second small bowl for the dressing.

**Chicken**

- Pat the chicken dry and salt it inside and out. Set it breast up in a cast-iron skillet, wings tucked under, with the shallots around it (with legs, nestle them under and around). Season the shallots with salt and pepper.
- Brush the anchovy mayo all over the chicken, into every nook, then brush the shallots with what's left.

**Roast**

- Put the skillet in with the legs toward the back of the oven. Roast until some mayo and fat drip onto the shallots, about 15 minutes. Turn the shallots in the drippings.
- Keep roasting, stirring the shallots once or twice, until golden brown and the thickest part of the breast reads 155°F, 45-55 minutes. Tent any darkening spots with foil. (Legs: start checking at 40 minutes; 160°F at the joint.)
- Move the chicken and shallots to a board, leaving the juices and fat in the skillet. If the shallots need more time, give them a few more minutes alone.

**Dressing**

- Lower the oven to 400°F. Finely grate half the zest of 1 lemon into a large bowl, then squeeze in its juice. Whisk in the reserved 1 TBLS anchovy mayo, the remaining 3 TBLS mayo and 1 TBLS oil, then stir in the Parmesan. Season with salt and pepper.

**Croutons and salad**

- Turn the bread in the fat left in the skillet. Toast in the oven, tossing halfway, until golden and crisp, 12-15 minutes.
- Add the croutons to the dressing, then the romaine, and toss gently until coated. Season.

**Serve**

- Cut the remaining lemon into wedges. Carve the chicken and arrange it with the shallots and lemon wedges. Serve with the salad and more mustard alongside.
