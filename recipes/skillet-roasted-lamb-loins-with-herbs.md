**Herb paste**

- In a small bowl, combine 2 TBLS of the oil with the garlic, shallot, rosemary, sage, marjoram and thyme.

**Roll and tie**

- Lay the loins on a work surface, fat side down, and season with salt and pepper. Spread the herb paste all over the lamb.
- Roll each loin over the tenderloin and rib apron to make a neat roulade. With butcher's twine, tie at 1-inch intervals. Season the outside with salt and pepper.

**Brown and roast**

- Heat the oven to 350°F. In a 12-inch skillet (cast iron if you have it), heat the remaining 1 TBLS oil until shimmering.
- Add the loins and cook over moderate heat (pan surface 300-350°F), turning, until browned all over, about 20 minutes total.
- Move the skillet to the oven and roast about 10 minutes, until an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part reads 125-130°F for medium-rare (it climbs about 5°F as it rests).
- Move the loins to a carving board and rest 10 minutes.

**Serve**

- Cut off the strings, carve into 1-inch-thick slices, and serve.
