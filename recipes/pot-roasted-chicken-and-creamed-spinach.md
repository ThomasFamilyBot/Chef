**Stuff and butter the chicken**

- Heat the oven to 480°F (250°C in the original).
- Put the chicken in a large oval cast iron pot. Salt the cavity, then stuff it with the onion, garlic, lemon and dried herbs from `1 -> Chicken`.
- Salt the bird all over. Smear the 8 TBLS soft butter thickly over the breast.

**Get the color**

- Put the pot in the oven, uncovered. Cook until the chicken has a deep, even color, taking it out a couple of times to baste it with the butter in the pot. The reel gives no time; expect about 25 to 30 minutes.

**Add the liquid and steam it through**

- Once it has the right color, pour in the white wine and chicken stock from `2 -> Pot liquid` and put the lid on.
- Turn the oven down to very low, about 210°F (100°C). Return the pot for about 25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and rested. The thickest part of the thigh should read 165°F; if it doesn't, give it longer with the lid on.
- Steaming it in the pot is what keeps it juicy and tender.
- Lift the chicken onto a tray. Pour the pot juices over it, then pour them off into a jug to serve as the sauce.

**Make the creamed spinach**

- In the same pot, over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F), add the olive oil, the 2 TBLS butter and the grated garlic from `3 -> Creamed spinach`. Cook the garlic off for a minute.
- Add the spinach and wilt it down quickly.
- Stir in the cream, cream cheese, a little salt and pepper, and the parmesan.

**Carve and serve**

- Carve the chicken into legs and breasts. Lay the pieces over the creamed spinach in the pot.
- Pour the sauce over at the table.
