**Sauté the veg**

- Medium high — broccoli, onion, 1/2 tsp Garlic Salt... sauté for 5 min (2 TBLS of water at end, if desired).

**Eggs**

- Eggs + 1/2 tsp Garlic Salt + 1/2 tsp Herbes de Provence.

**Sous vide**

- Sous vide to 172°F (60 min).
