**Spicy ginger syrup** (makes about 1 cup)

- Blend the ginger, chile, honey and 1/4 cup warm water until you can barely make out the chile pieces, about 1 minute.
- Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a resealable container, pressing on the solids to get everything out. Refrigerate until chilled, up to 1 week.

**Pitcher** (up to 4 hours before serving)

- Put the Thai basil leaves in the bottom of a 2- to 2 1/2-quart pitcher and add 1/2 cup bourbon. Muddle gently, tapping the leaves about 15 times without pulverizing them.
- Add the remaining 2 1/4 cups bourbon, stir, and let sit 15 minutes at room temperature.
- Stir in the spicy ginger syrup and 1 cup water. If not serving right away, seal well (plastic wrap if needed) and refrigerate.

**Lime** (up to 2 hours before serving)

- Stir the lime juice into the pitcher. Reseal and refrigerate if not serving right away.

**Serve**

- Fill the pitcher with ice and stir until the outside of the pitcher feels cold. Pour into ice-filled rocks glasses and garnish each with a Thai basil sprig.

**Notes**

- From a San Francisco bartender: fresh ginger and red Thai chile for real heat, Thai basil for a floral, anise-like lift. Fill each glass with ice for proper dilution and chill.
