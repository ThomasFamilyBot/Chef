**Brine**

- Whisk the fish sauce, water, shallots, garlic, chile, granulated sugar and pepper until the sugar dissolves. Add the chicken, cover, and refrigerate 4 hours.

**Sear and roast**

- Heat the oven to 400°F. Lift the chicken out, pat dry, and keep the brine and its aromatics.
- Heat a large ovenproof skillet over high heat (pan surface 450-500°F) for 2 minutes, add 1 TBLS oil, and lay the chicken in skin side down. After 1 minute, move the skillet to the oven and roast until cooked through, 18-20 minutes (160°F).

**Sauce**

- Strain the reserved brine through a fine sieve into a bowl. In a small saucepan over medium-high, cook the strained aromatics in the remaining 1 tsp oil until sizzling and soft, 1-2 minutes.
- Pour in the liquid and simmer until reduced by half, 2-3 minutes. Turn off the heat.

**Brûlée**

- Move the chicken skin side up to a rack on a sheet pan. Set a rack in the upper-middle position and heat the broiler to high.
- Sprinkle the turbinado over the skin and broil until glossy and browned, 2-3 minutes; watch it closely. (A kitchen torch works too.)

**Serve**

- Slice each breast crosswise 1/2 inch thick. Divide the sauce among 4 plates, add the chicken, and sprinkle with cilantro.

**Notes**

- Turbinado tastes less sweet than white sugar and takes longer to melt, so it caramelizes into a real crunch.
- The brine is cooked into the sauce, so it's counted in full.
