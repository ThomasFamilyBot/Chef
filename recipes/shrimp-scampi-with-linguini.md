**Pasta**

- Bring a large pot of water to a boil (212°F), add a couple of tablespoons of salt and the linguine. Stir so it separates; cover. When it returns to a boil, cook 6-8 minutes, until not quite done. Drain.

**Scampi**

- Meanwhile, in a large skillet over medium-high heat (pan surface 375-425°F), melt 2 TBLS butter in 2 TBLS oil.
- Sauté the shallots, garlic and red pepper flakes (if using) until the shallots are translucent, 3-4 minutes.
- Season the shrimp with salt and pepper, add to the pan, and cook until pink, 2-3 minutes. Take the shrimp out and keep warm.

**Finish**

- Add the wine and lemon juice and bring to a boil. Add the remaining 2 TBLS butter and 2 TBLS oil.
- When the butter melts, return the shrimp with the parsley and the pasta. Toss well, season with salt and pepper, drizzle with a little more oil, and serve immediately.
