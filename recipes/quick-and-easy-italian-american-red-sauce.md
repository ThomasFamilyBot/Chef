**Aromatics**

- Put the oil and garlic in a large pot cold and heat over medium-low heat (pan surface 250-300°F) until the garlic just starts to sizzle gently.
- Add the pepper flakes and cook, stirring, until the garlic is turning light golden, about 3 minutes. Add the oregano and cook 1 minute more.

**Tomato paste**

- Add the tomato paste and cook, stirring and mashing, until it softens and blends into the oil, about 3 minutes.

**Tomatoes**

- Stir in the tomatoes and juices, raise to medium-high, bring to a gentle simmer (185-205°F), then lower the heat to hold it there.
- Blend smooth with an immersion blender right in the pot. Or crush the tomatoes by hand before adding, or with a potato masher in the pot, for a chunkier sauce (that leaves garlic chunks too).

**Simmer & finish**

- Add the basil and simmer, stirring now and then, until slightly reduced and rich, about 30 minutes. Season with salt.
- Discard the basil. Stir in the butter, if using, until melted.
- Use right away, or cool and refrigerate up to 5 days or freeze up to 6 months.

**Notes**

- Makes the 5 cups needed for [The Best Italian-American Meatballs](https://thomasfamilybot.github.io/Chef/r/the-best-italian-american-meatballs.html).
- Reviewers found it mild as written: more garlic, more pepper flakes, or a Parmesan rind in with the basil are common tweaks.
