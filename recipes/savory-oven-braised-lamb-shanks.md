**Vegetables**

- Heat the oven to 325°F.
- In a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F), heat 1 TBLS of the oil with the butter. Add the onion, celery and carrot and cook, stirring, until the onion softens.
- Add the garlic and cook, stirring, 2 minutes more. Move the vegetables to a large baking dish or Dutch oven.

**Sear the shanks**

- Add the remaining 2 TBLS oil to the skillet. Season the shanks with salt and pepper and brown over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F), turning to sear all sides, about 8 minutes. Add them to the pan with the vegetables.

**Braising liquid**

- In the same skillet, deglaze with the red wine, scraping up the browned bits, and simmer 2 minutes.
- Add the tomato paste, chicken stock, beef stock and vinegar. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer 5 minutes. Pour over the shanks.

**Braise**

- Tuck in the thyme and bay leaf. Cover the pan tightly (lid or foil) and bake 1 1/2 hours.
- Uncover and keep baking 2-2 1/2 hours longer, turning the shanks now and then, until the meat is very tender and pulls from the bone (about 200°F internal).
- Discard the bay leaf and thyme stems. Serve with mashed potatoes and steamed vegetables.

**Notes**

- The source lists 3 hours 30 minutes cook time, but the oven alone runs 3 1/2-4 hours plus about 20 minutes on the stove; the times here follow the directions.
- Shanks run 3/4-1 1/4 lb each; 4 lb total is assumed. Nutrition counts the bone-in weight at the table's edible yield and all of the braising liquid as eaten; rendered fat isn't skimmed.
- Source's own estimate: 510 calories per serving.
