**Poach the pears**

- Put the water in a medium saucepan. Halve the vanilla bean lengthwise, scrape the seeds into the water, and add the pods and the sugar.
- Bring to a simmer (185-205°F) and simmer until the sugar dissolves.
- Add the pears and simmer 10 minutes, until soft when pierced and translucent on the surface.
- Off the heat, stir in the honey, corn syrup and lemon juice. Cool, then remove the vanilla pods.

**Blend and chill**

- Blend the pears with their syrup until smooth. Chill 2 hours or overnight.

**Freeze**

- Chill a container in the freezer. Blend the pear mixture again for 30 seconds with an immersion blender.
- Freeze in an ice cream maker per the manufacturer's directions. Pack into the chilled container and freeze at least 2 hours.

**Notes**

- Wait until the pears are properly ripe; a brown paper bag with an apple speeds that up.
