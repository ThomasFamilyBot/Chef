**Season the chops**

- Heat the sous vide bath to 142°F for medium (little to no pink, juicy). Other targets: 139-140°F medium-rare and pink; 150°F medium-well; 160°F well done and a bit dry.
- Season the chops on both sides with salt, pepper and garlic powder.

**Marinade**

- Combine everything in `2 -> Marinade` in a glass measuring cup (about 1/2 cup).
- Put the chops in a large zip-top bag (or two) or a vacuum bag. Pour in half the marinade (1/4 cup) and massage to coat. Keep the other half for the glaze.

**Cook sous vide**

- Seal by water displacement: lower the open bag into the water until the top is just above the surface, then zip it; it should sink.
- Cook 2-2 1/2 hours for chops 1 1/4-1 1/2 inches thick; 1-1 1/2 hours for thin chops under an inch. There's about an hour of leeway.

**Glaze**

- While the chops cook, stir the lime juice, garlic and cornstarch into the reserved marinade. Microwave 45-60 seconds to thicken; loosen with a splash of water if it's too thick.

**Finish on the grill**

- Heat the grill to high (grate 500-550°F). Take the chops out of the bag and pat dry.
- Brush glaze on both sides and the edges, holding back 1 TBLS. Oil the grate.
- Sear 40-60 seconds per side for grill marks, lid closed between flips. (Or broil 6 inches from the element about a minute a side until bubbly and charred in spots, or sear a minute a side in a hot cast-iron pan, glazing at the end.)
- Brush with the last of the glaze, scatter with cilantro, and serve.

**Notes**

- No fish sauce? 2-2 1/2 tsp Worcestershire or soy sauce instead.
- Make ahead: the marinade keeps 1-2 days; the chops can marinate up to a day. Cooked chops can be ice-bathed in the bag, refrigerated 1-2 days, and rewarmed at 130°F for 45-60 minutes.
