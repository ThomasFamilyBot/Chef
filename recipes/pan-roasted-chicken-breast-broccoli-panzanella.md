**Broccoli**

- Heat the oven to 425°F.
- Toss the florets with 2 TBLS of the oil, salt and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet and put in the oven.

**Chicken**

- Heat an ovenproof sauté pan over high heat (pan surface 450-500°F). Pat the chicken dry and season both sides.
- Add 2 TBLS oil; when it smokes, lay in the chicken skin side down. After 30 seconds, move the pan to the oven alongside the broccoli. (No ovenproof pan? Transfer the chicken to a roasting dish.)
- Roast 5-6 minutes, until the skin is browned, bubbly and crisp, then turn the chicken and the broccoli. Roast about 6 minutes more, until just cooked through (160°F). Set both aside on a warm plate; the broccoli should be browned in places. Cut it into bite-size pieces.

**Panzanella**

- In a large bowl, mix the lemon juice and zest, anchovy paste and garlic, then whisk in the remaining 3 TBLS oil.
- Add the red pepper flakes, basil and red onion, then the bread cubes and broccoli, and toss. Taste and adjust.

**Serve**

- Grate Parmesan over the panzanella and serve alongside the chicken.

**Notes**

- Scales up easily; roast the broccoli and chicken on separate sheets if you double it.
