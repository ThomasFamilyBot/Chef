**Soften the onion & garlic**

- Heat 2 TBLS of the oil in a large skillet over medium heat (pan surface about 300-350°F).
- Add the onion, oregano and 1/2 tsp salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly browned, 5-7 minutes.
- Stir in the garlic and cook until lightly golden, 1-2 minutes.

**Reduce the wine**

- Stir in 1/2 cup of the wine and cook until nearly evaporated, about 2 minutes.
- Add the remaining 1/2 cup and reduce (steady simmer, 190-205°F) by at least half, until a spatula pulled through leaves a trail, about 6 minutes.

**Simmer the tomatoes**

- Stir in the crushed tomatoes and honey. Bring to a simmer (185-205°F) and cook until slightly thickened, about 10 minutes.

**Finish**

- Off the heat, stir in the remaining 1 TBLS oil and the basil. Season with more basil, salt and pepper to taste.

**Serve**

- Toss with cooked whole-wheat pasta: 1 lb for 4 people (refrigerate or freeze the rest of the sauce), 1 1/2-2 lb for 6-8.

**Notes**

- Keeps 5 days in the fridge or 4 months frozen in zipper-lock bags.
- Nutrition is for the sauce only; the pasta is not counted.
