**Base**

- In a bowl, whisk the milk and sugar until the sugar dissolves. Stir in the cream and peppermint extract.
- Turn on the ice cream maker, pour in the base, and churn about 25 minutes.

**Chips**

- During the last 5 minutes of churning, slowly add the chopped chocolate.
- Pack into an airtight container and freeze at least 2-3 hours before serving.

**Notes**

- A few drops of green food coloring are optional; the flavor is the same without.
- Swap vanilla extract for the peppermint and it's a plain chocolate chip ice cream.
