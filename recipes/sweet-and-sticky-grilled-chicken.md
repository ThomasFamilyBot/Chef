**Season the chicken**

- Season the halves generously all over with salt (about 1 tsp Diamond Crystal per pound) and pepper. Sit at room temperature at least 15 minutes, or chill up to a day; if chilled, bring to room temperature 1 hour before grilling.

**Glaze**

- Whisk the marmalade, mustard, vinegar, soy sauce, jalapeño (if using) and garlic. Set aside.

**Grill**

- Set up the grill for medium-high indirect heat: coals banked to one side, or one or two gas burners off. Oil the grate.
- Pat the chicken dry and rub with 1 TBLS oil. Set skin side down over the indirect side, cover, and grill, turning halfway, until the skin is lightly browned and the thighs read 120-130°F, 15-20 minutes.
- Uncover, flip, and move over direct heat (grate 400-450°F). Brush with glaze and grill, turning often and brushing generously (back to indirect if it browns too fast), until charred in spots and the breast reads 150°F (it climbs to 160°F as it rests), 10-15 minutes.
- Rest skin side up on a board 15 minutes.

**Sauce and serve**

- While the chicken rests, cook any remaining glaze in a small saucepan over medium-high, stirring, until bubbling and slightly thickened, about 5 minutes.
- Carve, sprinkle with flaky salt, and serve with the sauce.
