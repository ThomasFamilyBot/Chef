**Get ready**

- Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9-by-13-inch baking dish.

**Cook the pasta**

- Fill a large pot three-quarters full with water and salt it generously. Bring to a boil (212°F).
- Cook the pasta to al dente by the package directions, or 1 minute less than the full time. It keeps cooking in the oven, so don't let it get soft. Drain and set aside.

**Mix the cheese**

- Combine everything from `2 -> Cheese` in a large bowl. You should have about 10 cups.

**Make the cheese sauce**

- In a large skillet over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F), melt the butter. Add the smoked paprika, salt, garlic powder and black pepper and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
- Add the flour and keep stirring until it becomes a thick paste, 3 to 5 minutes.
- Slowly whisk in the evaporated milk, a little at a time, and keep whisking until it thickens, about 5 minutes. Whisk in the heavy cream and mustard.
- Turn the heat to low. Slowly whisk in 2 1/2 cups of the grated cheese and let it melt, then whisk in another 2 1/2 cups. About 5 cups stay in the bowl.
- Be patient here. If the heat is too high the sauce breaks and turns grainy. Keep whisking until creamy and smooth.
- Stir in the cooked pasta until it is completely coated.

**Layer and bake**

- In the buttered dish, add a layer of cheesy pasta, then a layer of grated cheese. Keep layering, ending with cheese on top, until all the pasta and cheese are used.
- Bake until the cheese is melted and bubbly, 25 to 30 minutes. Take the dish out.
- Move the oven rack 6 inches from the broiler, or lower if the dish needs the room, and heat the broiler to high.
- Broil until the top is well colored, about 2 minutes. Watch it closely so it doesn't burn.
- Sprinkle with the chives, if using.

**Notes**

- Grate the cheese yourself. Pregrated cheese has anti-clumping additives that keep it from melting well and can make the sauce grainy.
- Make ahead: do everything up to baking, including the cheese on top, a day in advance. Cover tightly and refrigerate. Take it out 30 minutes before baking; too cold and the cheese can separate and turn greasy. Bake as written.
- To freeze: the same, frozen for up to 3 months. Thaw in the fridge the day before, then take it out 30 minutes before baking.
- Leftovers keep up to 4 days in an airtight container. Reheat covered with foil, with a splash of milk to loosen the pasta.
