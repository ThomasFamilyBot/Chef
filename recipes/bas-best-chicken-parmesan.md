**Tomato sauce** (up to 2 days ahead)

- Heat 1/2 cup oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F). Add the onion and crushed garlic and cook, stirring often, until softened but not browned, about 5 minutes.
- Add the tomato paste and red pepper flakes and cook, stirring, until the paste darkens and starts to fry in the oil, about 1 minute.
- Add the tomato purée, salt and sugar and bring to a simmer. Partially cover, reduce to a bare simmer (180-190°F), and cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 40-50 minutes.

**Cutlets and marinade**

- Butterfly each breast: knife parallel to the board, slice along the long side to within 1/2 inch of the other edge, open like a book, and pound between wax paper to 1/3 inch thick.
- Combine the grated garlic, lemon juice and 1/4 cup olive oil in a large baking dish. Season the cutlets all over with salt (about 3/4 tsp Diamond Crystal each), add to the marinade, and turn to coat. Sit 20 minutes to 1 hour; the lemon turns the flesh opaque, which is fine.

**Dredge**

- Whisk the eggs, garlic powder, onion powder, 1/2 tsp salt and 2 TBLS water in a shallow bowl. Panko in a second bowl; flour whisked with 1 tsp salt in a third.
- One at a time, letting excess marinade drip off, dredge in flour, dip in egg, then press firmly into panko with no bare spots. Set on a rimmed baking sheet and chill 20 minutes to 8 hours.

**Cheese**

- Set a wire rack in a second rimmed sheet. Toss the grated mozzarella with the Parmesan.

**Fry**

- Pour 1 inch of oil into a large high-sided skillet and heat over medium to 400°F. In batches, returning the oil to 400°F each time, fry the cutlets until deep golden brown, about 2 minutes per side. Move to the rack and salt.

**Broil**

- Heat the broiler. Arrange the cutlets in two large baking dishes. Spoon sauce generously over each, leaving some corners and edges bare, and spoon the rest around them. Cover with the cheese, again leaving crispy edges showing.
- Broil until the cheese is melted, bubbling and browned in spots, about 4 minutes. Cool slightly and scatter with parsley.

**Notes**

- Nutrition counts the breading, marinade and frying oil at the house partial-use rates (see the line notes).
