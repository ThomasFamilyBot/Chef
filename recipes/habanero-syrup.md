**Make the syrup**

- Combine everything from `1 -> Make the syrup` in a small saucepan and bring to a simmer (185-205°F), stirring to dissolve the sugar.

**Finish the syrup**

- Remove from the heat and stir in `2 -> Finish the syrup`.
- Let stand for at least 1 hour before using to bring out the flavors.
- Keeps up to 1 week refrigerated in a covered container.
