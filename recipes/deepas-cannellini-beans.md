**The night before**

- Soak the beans from `1 -> Soak` overnight in plenty of water with enough salt to make it briny.
- Rinse and drain the beans before cooking.

**Start the pot**

- Set a heavy pot (Deepa uses a Fissler pressure cooker) over medium heat (pan surface 300-350°F).
- Add the olive oil from `2 -> Start the pot`. Optional: add the rendered fat of a slice of bacon.
- Add the fennel seeds and chopped onion. Cook 4 to 5 minutes, until the onion is soft and turns light brown.

**Cook the vegetables**

- Add everything from `3 -> Vegetables` (not the garlic). Sauté for 5 minutes.

**Add the Madeira**

- Add the wine from `4 -> Madeira`. Mix well and let it reduce.
- Move the chopped vegetables to the side of the pot.

**Fry the tomato paste**

- Add the tomato paste from `5 -> Tomato paste` to the middle of the pot. Sauté it, adding a little butter or olive oil, until the paste turns a deep maroon color.
- Add the minced garlic, then the herbs and spices, all from `6 -> Garlic, herbs and spices`.

**Add the beans**

- Add the soaked, rinsed and drained beans.
- Add the Parmesan rind from `7 -> Beans`.
- Add the broth and bring to a simmer (185-205°F).

**Cook until soft**

- Pressure cooker: lock the lid and pressure-cook for 3 to 4 minutes.
- Or on the stove: simmer as the bean package says, normally 2 hours or more, stirring occasionally.
- Either way, the beans are done when they are soft.

**Season**

- Remove the Parmesan rind.
- Add the sea salt flakes from `8 -> Season` to taste.

**Top and serve**

- Heat the olive oil from `9 -> Topping` in a saucepan. Lightly brown the sliced garlic and the Aleppo pepper or chili flakes.
- Pour it over the beans. Serve with shaved Parmesan on top.

**Notes**

- From Deepa's typed sheet. The wording and the cooking are hers.
- ChefBot filled in three things the sheet leaves out, each marked (est.): the amount of minced garlic for the pot, how many it serves, and the times. Ask Deepa and they get corrected.
- The butter or olive oil for frying the tomato paste and the optional bacon fat have no amounts on the sheet, so they are not in the ingredient table.
- ChefBot's addition, pressure cooker: the 3 to 4 minutes starts when the cooker is up to pressure. The sheet doesn't say how to release it; letting the pressure come down on its own keeps the beans from splitting. If they are still firm, simmer with the lid off until soft.
- ChefBot's addition, stove: cannellini are white kidney beans, so boil them hard for 10 minutes before turning down to a simmer. The pressure cooker takes care of this by itself.
