**Toss the apples**

- Heat the oven to 400°F. Line a large baking sheet with parchment, foil or a silicone mat.
- In a large bowl, toss everything in `1 -> Toss the apples` until the apples are well coated.
- Scrape onto the baking sheet in a single layer.

**Dot with butter and roast**

- Scatter the butter cubes from `2 -> Dot with butter` over the tray.
- Roast 10 minutes, stir, then roast another 5-10 minutes, until the apples are soft and golden but not mushy.
- Serve warm, cold or at room temperature.

**Notes**

- Use apples that are tart and hold their shape when baked: Granny Smith or Jonagold.
- Swap or add spices: cardamom, allspice, ginger or nutmeg, a little at a time.
- Keeps about 3 days in an airtight container in the fridge.
