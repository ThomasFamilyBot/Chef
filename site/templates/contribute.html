Send us a recipe

Cooked something we should have? Send it in and it gets added to the library with a printable Google Doc, a shopping-list entry and whole-recipe nutrition. You'll get the links back by email.

By email

Send to {{ site.submission_email }} with any of these:

  • A link to the recipe online. That's enough on its own.
  • The recipe typed out: ingredients with amounts, then the steps. Tell us where it came from (a cookbook and page, a friend, your own).
  • Your name, so the recipe can credit you.

Photos or scans

Email can't carry photos for us, so drop cookbook pages or handwritten cards into the shared folder Chef Inbox. Name the file with the recipe and your name, for example Grandma's pierogi - Sam.jpg. JPG, HEIC, PNG and PDF all work.

What happens next

  • Submissions are picked up on a schedule, usually within a day.
  • The recipe is converted to the house format: a one-page ingredient table with bowl groupings, directions with temperatures, and nutrition for the whole recipe.
  • You get an email with the recipe page and the Google Doc. If something was unclear, the email says what was assumed.

Please only send recipes you're happy to share with the family and friends who use this site.
